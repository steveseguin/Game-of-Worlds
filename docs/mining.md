# Mining expeditions

Use Mining Haulers to earn metal and crystal from planets you cannot yet settle.

1. Select your Spaceport, open **Fleet**, and build a **Mining Hauler**. Its base price is 300 metal and 3 production; no research is required.
2. Select an unclaimed planet and choose **Move Ships**. Select your hauler and review the route's fuel cost and hazards before confirming.
3. Leave it there until the turn ends. It automatically loads 100 resources, even if the planet requires more Terraforming than you have.
4. Use **Plot return** in the Fleet manifest to select the haulers and a return planet, then review and confirm the route. You can also use **Move Ships** to choose any owned planet. Leave it there until the turn ends to add its cargo to your treasury.
5. Send it out again. The **Mining expeditions** manifest in Fleet shows each location, cargo, and next operation. Select a sector in the manifest to find the ships on the map.

A typical hold carries 60 metal and 40 crystal. Metal-rich worlds shift that mix toward metal; crystal-rich worlds shift it toward crystal. The total remains 100. Loading and unloading each take a turn, so a complete round trip takes at least two turns.

Haulers use the same plotted routes and crystal fuel costs as other ships. They are unarmed: escort them through hostile territory. Black holes destroy them, unsecured asteroid belts can destroy them, and destroyed ships lose their cargo. Combat resolves before cargo operations; a sector containing rival fleets cannot load or unload.

Mining does not claim the planet, use a Colony Ship, or produce research. Once somebody settles the world, it stops being a mining destination. Full holds cannot load more; an empty hauler at an owned planet waits for your next order. Cargo aboard ships is not spendable until delivery.
