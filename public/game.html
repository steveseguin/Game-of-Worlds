Command Reference

REF-00
  • Turns advance on the clock in the top right; press End Turn to finish yours early.
  • Resources. Build extractors and refineries on sectors you own; research unlocks tech and better hulls. Crystal is what you spend to find things out — probes, movement and spycraft all come out of it.
  • Look before you fly. A ship above light speed cannot see what is ahead of it, so an unexplored sector is a guess. A probe costs 300 crystal, reveals a sector without risking a fleet, and is offered on the sector panel when you select somewhere you have never been. A probe can still be lost — it arrives like a ship does, so a black hole always destroys it and an unswept belt has about one chance in four; an empire whose space you probe may also shoot it down.
  • Asteroid belts are the risk you can retire. Crossing one kills about half the hulls that enter it; stopping in one kills about a quarter. Take it and it is safe forever after, and it pays ore — the cheapest permanent thing in the game.
  • Black holes are not a risk, they are an ending. No roll, no survivors, no exceptions, whoever owns the sector. Route around them; that is what the map key's black-hole mark is for.
  • Colonize. Build a colony ship in the Fleet tab, move it to an unowned planet, then settle it from the Colonize tab — which lists exactly which of the four requirements you are still missing. Settling consumes the ship.
  • Moving a fleet. Click a sector on the map, then press Move Ships Here in the Sector Survey panel on the left. The survey also names the sector's type and, once you have been there, its yields.
  • Combat. Fleets in the same sector resolve automatically each turn; build scouts early for vision.
  • How you win. The victory line under the treasury tracks five races at once: Conquest (hold most of the galaxy's planets), Survival (be the last empire standing), Wealth, Research (finish your race's whole tech tree) and Score when the turn limit runs out. Each of those five names on the bar is a term: point at it, tap it, or Tab to it for the exact target. Analytics ▸ Victory Briefing has the standings, and lists the same five under their formal names.
  • Keyboard. Press M to jump to the tactical chart — arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects, and a second M hands focus back to where you were. Press ? or F1 any time for this panel — or the ? key in the top right — and close it with Esc.
