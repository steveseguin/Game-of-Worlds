# The Master Timeline

Status: **CANON.** This document outranks every other file in `lore/` on any question of date, sequence,
or number. Where another document disagrees with this one, this one is right and the other is a bug.

Dates in **AU / BU** — *After / Before the Unarriving*. The present, and the first turn of every match,
is **AU 74**.

---

## Part 1 — The continuity audit

`lore/` was written across nine passes at speed, and this was the first pass to actually check it. **Ten
real contradictions were found and all ten are now fixed.** Logged here so nobody re-introduces them.

**Two things that looked wrong and are not**, recorded so they are not "fixed" by mistake:

- **The Quiet Decade lasted sixty-one years.** This is an in-world misnomer, not an error — see the era
  entry below. Do not rename it.
- **The Shadow Realm held the imagery for thirteen years before selling any of it.** BU 13 to roughly
  AU 0. Correct: before the Unarriving there was no market for a picture of some Ancients doing
  maintenance. The Unarriving created the market.

## Part 2 — The chronology

### Before records — the Long Trellis

Every civilisation's oldest surviving document already assumes the Trellis. Twelve species, on twelve
separate timelines, each reached the stars and found the roads already swept and already lit. **There is no
discovery narrative anywhere in the galaxy.** The Ancients maintained it. They did not build it, and that
distinction is the campaign's central revelation and is never confirmed until forced.

The Crystalline Entity is the only witness older than every other civilisation's records, and all it can
say is that the Trellis was *already there*, because it had no reason to be paying attention.

### BU 61 to BU 0 — the Quiet Decade

**It lasted sixty-one years and everyone calls it a decade.** The name comes from the last ten, when the
failures became impossible to ignore; popular memory compressed six decades of creeping fault into the
period when people finally noticed. Histories misname eras. Keep it.

| Date | Race | Event |
|---|---|---|
| **BU 61** | Ancients | **They begin the shutdown.** The failure front starts propagating outward in a shell from a point in empty space |
| BU 61→ | Silicon | Their survey data from this period is the only continuous record, and the reason they alone can see the sequence |
| BU 40 | Bioform | **The hull-lines stop taking on the deep lanes.** Not sickening — *thin*, slow in the berth. They reroute over eleven years and tell nobody |
| BU 13 | Ancients | Eleven Ancient vessels work at the relay beside a collapsar, for some months |
| BU 13 | Shadow Realm | They record it. They hold the imagery, unsold, for thirteen years — there is no market for it yet |
| **BU 8** | Ancients | **The four sentences.** Sent to all twelve empires at once, asking for reduced transit volume, no reason given |
| BU 8 | Terran | Clerk Ames files it *Commercial — Shipping — Advisory*, correctly, twenty minutes before the end of her shift. She reads it twice |
| BU 2 | Titan Lords | The *Weight of Consideration* is launched |
| BU 2→ | Titan Lords | Shipmaster Vaun takes command and holds it forty-six years |

> **The fact this timeline exists to surface, and which no other document in `lore/` states plainly:**
>
> **The shutdown began in BU 61. The warning came in BU 8.**
>
> The Ancients said nothing for **fifty-three years** while the thing they had started propagated across
> the galaxy — and when they finally spoke, it was four sentences, with no reason given, to a shipping
> clerk. That is the most damning fact in the setting and it does not require the Shadow Realm's imagery,
> or Nine's analysis, or anybody's testimony. It is just arithmetic on two dates that were already canon.

### The clock — the galaxy's one piece of rising pressure

Recorded here because it was implicit in four documents and stated in none, which is why the report
card graded **Escalation C+** with the note *"nothing is coming."* Something is, and it is countable:

| | |
|---|---|
| **AU 12** | Four heavy Mechanicus hulls enter 9-D. Nothing returns. Annotation: *requires heavier hull. Revisit.* With a **review interval** |
| **AU 68** | The Ancients' watch logs seven patched hulls passing the relay **at one sector**, no reconnaissance screen, three-fifths speed, annotating as they go, **on a schedule.** Reported to every installation still answering. Eleven answer. **Nobody is dispatched** |
| **AU 74** | Yard Nine's variance is accepted. The annotation is due *this decade* |
| **Then** | The Endless Yard arrives at 9-D, having not looked, because looking was never on the order |

**Why this is the right shape of threat for this setting.** It is not a villain, an invasion, or an
awakening. It is **a work order with no completion condition, arriving on time** — filed by units that
no longer exist, executed by units that were fabricated centuries later, and unstoppable because the
Second Shift has no office that can cancel anything. The Ancients saw it coming six years ago, told
everyone, and could not act.

The player first reads the clock in Act One, mission 3 (`22-act-one.md`): a stamped plate on a rock at
the northern margin, two hundred years old, with a review interval on it.

## Part 3 — Rules for keeping this straight

1. **BU counts down.** BU 61 is *earlier* than BU 8. This produced two of the ten errors above and it will
   produce more. When writing a "X years before Y" claim, do the subtraction.
2. **Check the recording date against every claim inside a testimony.** A witness cannot cite a document
   published after their interview. This produced errors 1, 3 and 6.
3. **The present is AU 74 and every duration must resolve against it.** *"Seventy-four years"* means
   since the Unarriving. It does not mean "a long time," and it is wrong for anything that did not start
   in AU 0.
4. **When an event involves two races, both files must be changed together.** Error 8 sat in six files on
   the Shadow side and two on the Ancient side, describing the same afternoon in BU 13.
5. **New dated claims go in this table first**, then into the prose file. A date that exists only in a
   testimony is a date nobody will check again.
