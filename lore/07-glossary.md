# Glossary

Status: **PROPOSED**. This file is the naming authority. When two documents disagree about a
word, this one wins; when a word starts appearing in two spellings, the world starts feeling
fake.

---

## Core terms

**The Trellis** — the ancient network of surveyed, swept, lit lanes between the stars. Nobody
alive built it. The Ancients maintained it. It is called the Trellis because twelve
civilisations grew on it the way vines grow on a frame, and discovered when it was removed that
they could not stand up.

**The Lamps** — the beacons that gave a ship at superluminal speed advance knowledge of the
space ahead. A lit lane was safe. Their operating principle is unknown and stays unknown.
*Register note: "the Lamps" is domestic and warm on purpose. "The Lamps are out" should sound
like something said about a house.*

**The Unarriving** — AU 0. The nine days in which every ship in superluminal transit failed to
arrive. Always described as an absence, never an explosion. **The number lost is not known,
because the count was kept by the Trellis.** *Formal, written term only — in speech, characters
say "the nine days" or "when the Lamps went out." See the register note below.*

**The Shortening** — AU 0–30. The logistical collapse that followed, which killed far more
people than the Unarriving did, and which nobody memorialises because famine has no enemy in it.

**The Sweeping** — AU 30–present. The era of buying safe space one sector at a time, in hulls.
The game's present tense.

**The Relighting** — the current galaxy-wide project to rebuild a Lamp. Every major power has a
programme. None has succeeded. Most are secret.

**AU / BU** — *After / Before the Unarriving.* The only calendar all twelve empires share.
Present day is **AU 74**.

## Navigation

**Running dark** — travelling superluminal without a Lamp, i.e. blind. What everybody does now.

**A trace** — a route somebody has flown and survived. The most valuable object in the galaxy.
Property: inherited, sold, stolen, forged. *(The engine has always called them this —
`traceDirectRoute()`.)*

**A clean trace** — one flown at least once with no losses.

**A clean crossing** — a transit with no losses. The highest praise a Terran officer gives.

**A shoal** — an asteroid belt. At relativistic closing speed, gravel is ordnance. 50% loss per
hull crossing, 25% arriving, 0% departing.

**A swept shoal** — one you hold: surveyed, cleared, threaded with a marked corridor. Safe
forever, and it yields ore. The strategic soul of the game.

**A mouth** — a collapsed star. Formally *collapsar*; nobody says collapsar. Not a hazard, an
absence: no roll, no report, no survivors. Every mouth on every chart was placed there because
a fleet was sent and none came back.

**Salting** — feeding an enemy probe a false trace so that it flies where the trace says.
What the engine calls "counter-intel destroys probes outright." Considered lawful by most
admiralties and obscene by the Star Nomads, who hold a funeral rite for a salted probe.

**The Deep** — unlit, uncharted space. What lies past the swept pockets. Not a place, a
condition.

## The Whisper

**The Whisper** — the surviving Trellis relay net. The Trellis had two layers: the **Lamps**, which
let ships see, and the **relays**, which carried signal. The Lamps are gone. The relays still work,
and nobody knows why one layer failed and the other did not.

It carries voice, text and ledgers instantly, anywhere. It carries **no cargo, no people, and no
sight** — it cannot show you a sector or guide a ship. It is not private, and everyone assumes
everyone listens, and everyone is right.

This is the rule that makes galactic politics possible: twelve empires can negotiate, trade, insult
each other and maintain a shared archive while being physically unable to reach one another. It is
also the setting's cruellest joke. **You can hear the whole galaxy and you cannot go anywhere.**
Families separated in AU 0 have been talking for seventy-four years. They have never met.

An order to your own fleet crosses the Whisper instantly. The fleet still takes seasons to arrive.
Command is fast, movement is not, and that gap is the texture of the whole war. See Law 11 in
`11-laws-of-the-world.md`.

## Materials

**Metal** — bulk. Hulls, structures, ordnance. Deliberately unmysterious.

**Crystal** — mined raw; refined it is called **reckoning**. Consumed by exactly three things:
probes, fleet movement, and spycraft — which are not three systems but one. *Crystal is what you
burn to know where you are.* The Trellis and the Lamps were made of it, which means every empire
funds its navigation by mining the corpse of the thing that used to keep it safe. Nobody has
proved this. Everybody suspects it.

**Reckoning** — refined crystal. Mass noun: *"we are out of reckoning."* The double meaning is
intentional and should never be pointed at.

## Institutions and ranks

**The Registry** — the Terran-maintained archive of standards, measures, casualty rolls, ship
plans and verified traces. Has no authority over anyone and sets the galactic standard anyway.
Also the in-world source of the twelve races' official names — which is why the game's race
list is a *document*, and an incomplete one.

**Registry name / endonym** — what the Terran archive calls a race, versus what it calls itself.
Registry names are the strings shipped in `races.js` and are **LOCKED**.

**Chart-Warden** — the officer who reconciles what left with what arrived, keeps the trace
ledger, and signs the notifications. A career for the meticulous. Expanded enormously after
AU 0. Rell's rank.

**Sweep-Admiral** — Terran officer commanding expansion into the Deep.

**The Registrar** — Terran head of state; formally the chief archivist. The office was never
meant to rule and acquired power during the Shortening because it was the only thing still
working.

**A Lamp-work** — a Lamp under construction. The Galactic Wonder. Currently unbuilt by anyone.

## Currencies

Nobody agreed on money. Full detail in `12-civilisations/README.md`.

**the stamp** *(Terra)* — a Registry certification seal. The galactic reserve currency, backed by
nothing but shared measurement · **the chit** *(Nomads)* — bearer note redeemable at any Fleet berth,
backed by somebody who might not arrive; the galaxy's first money · **the charge** *(Bell)* — a
courier's fee and a unit of obligation, personal and **inherited** · **the consideration** *(Titans)*
— a contract denominated in service life; a hull sold in AU 12 still pays · **duration**
*(Crystalline)* — agreements in centuries with terms nobody else can honour · **the season**
*(Bioform)* — growth cycles; nine years is the minimum serious horizon · **the work order**
*(Mechanicus)* — a thing is worth the shifts it takes · **mass** *(Zephyr)* — trade by weight ·
**owed answers** *(Shadow)* — debt in questions you must answer truthfully when asked; transferable
and inheritable · **repairs** *(Ancients)* — they fix a thing for you, and there is no exchange rate.

One line each; the civilisation files hold the detail.

| **the held question** | Sill | A model kept without hope of verification. The nearest thing they have to faith; asking is deeply intimate |

## Phrases in common use

- **"The world we were left on"** — one's homeworld. Idiom, not poetry: no empire chose its
  capital; every homeworld is where its people happened to be standing when the Lamps went out.
- **"Did not arrive"** — the standard formulation for lost crews. Ships are destroyed; people
  do not arrive. Rell's verbal habit and the project's most valuable single word choice.
- **"Requires heavier hull"** — Mechanicus annotation for a sector that killed a fleet. Chilling
  because it is a work order.
- **"It is on the chart now"** — said after a loss. The closest thing the galaxy has to a
  consolation.

## Vocabulary — **LOCKED** 2026-07-25

All six terms I had flagged as uncertain are kept. Reasoning recorded so nobody reopens it:

| Term | Kept because |
|---|---|
| **the Trellis** | Carries the metaphor exactly: everyone grew on a frame they did not build, and fell over when it was removed. Beat *the Lattice*, which is used everywhere in the genre |
| **the Unarriving** | Survivors really do name atrocities by nominalising an absence — *the Disappeared*, *the Troubles*. Being slightly hard to say is correct; people stumble over these words |
| **reckoning** | Calculation and comeuppance at once. Never pointed at, so the double meaning stays subliminal |
| **a mouth** | Plain *on purpose*. Navigators do not get poetic about the thing that eats their friends |
| **salting** | The word was already waiting for us: salting a mine means planting false ore to defraud an inspection. Salting a probe defrauds a navigator |
| **Chart-Warden** | Conveys the entire job in two words, which is what a rank should do |

### The one refinement: formal versus domestic

**"The Unarriving" is the written, formal term.** In speech, characters mostly say **"the nine
days"** or **"when the Lamps went out."**

Real vocabularies work this way — every community has a formal name and a household name for the
same catastrophe, and which one a person reaches for tells you their distance from it. Registry
documents say *the Unarriving*. Rell says *the nine days*. A Nomad says *when the Lamps went
out*, and means their family.

It also means a performer rarely has to say the difficult word, which is the practical half of
the reason.

**A shoal** and **running dark** were never in doubt. They do the maritime work the setting wants
and they survive contact with an actor's mouth.

## Terms we do not use

- *Hyperspace, subspace, jump drive, warp core.* Genre furniture. We have lanes, transit, and
  running dark.
- *Empire* as a compliment. Everyone is an empire here, and it is an administrative description.
- *Ancient evil / darkness / the void* as names for the thing behind the quarantine. It is never
  named. If it acquires a name, it acquires a shape, and it must not have one.
- *The Void* as a place. The Void Walkers are named for what they cross, and capitalising it
  elsewhere would confuse the roster.
