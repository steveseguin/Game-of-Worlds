# The Twelve Civilisations

Status: **PROPOSED**. Every stat, cap and hull restriction cited is **LOCKED** by `races.js`.
Everything here obeys `../11-laws-of-the-world.md`, which wins any conflict.

Twelve worlds, built geography-first. Terrain dictates where cities can exist; cities dictate what
the economy can be; the economy dictates who holds power; power dictates culture; culture dictates
what the place smells like. Each file follows that chain, and each one ends by explaining its race's
stat line — because if the worldbuilding doesn't produce the numbers already in the code, it is
decoration.

## The galactic economy

Not one of the twelve is self-sufficient, and the dependency graph is where every war starts.

| They have | They need | Which makes them |
|---|---|---|
| **Terra** — certification, paper, grain reserve | Everything else, moderately | Indispensable and unloved |
| **Sill** — actuarial certainty, forecasting | Power, silicate feedstock | Un-starvable, switch-off-able |
| **Churn** — bulk metal, hulls at volume | Crystal, knowledge | The cheapest supplier and worst counterparty |
| **Sarn's World** — the galaxy's best reckoning | Almost nothing | The largest unexploded device in politics |
| **Bell** — speed, route knowledge | **Food** | Fast, proud, and underfed |
| **the Works** — hulls, heavy metal | Nothing at all | The only genuinely autarkic economy |
| **Ossa's Delta** — living hulls, **food** | Metal | The larder that cannot surge production |
| **Kettering** — carriage, traces, the chit | Everything, constantly | The largest population with no vote |
| **the Ancients** — repairs beyond anyone's skill | A supply chain that stopped | Spending an inheritance |
| **Osk's Flux** — hedges, exotic lattices | Trust | Never accused of fraud successfully |
| **Ordel Deep** — capital hulls that last a century | Crystal, variety | Late to every war, undefeated in battle |
| **Sable's Dark** — fragments | Everything physical | A bluff that has never been called |

**Three chokepoints worth designing missions around.** Bell's food dependency — the fastest people in
the galaxy can be starved. The Bioform nine-year growth cycle — the larder cannot respond to a war.
And the reckoning market, where every empire's navigation depends on buying refined fragments of a
living people, from that people, and the faction that wants to stop selling is winning.

### Currencies

Nobody agreed on money, and the disagreements are characterisation.

| | Instrument | What it really is |
|---|---|---|
| Terra | **the stamp** | A certification seal. The reserve currency, backed by nothing but shared measurement |
| Kettering | **the chit** | A bearer note redeemable at any Fleet berth. Backed by somebody who might not arrive |
| Bell | **the charge** | A courier's fee, and a unit of obligation. Personal, and **inherited** |
| Ordel Deep | **the consideration** | A contract denominated in service life. A hull sold in AU 12 still pays |
| Sarn's World | **duration** | Agreements in centuries, with terms nobody else can honour. Their only joke |
| Ossa's Delta | **the season** | Growth cycles. Nine years is the minimum serious horizon |
| the Works | **the work order** | A thing is worth the shifts it takes. No market, no prices |
| Churn | **mass** | Trade by weight. No internal property at all |
| Sable's Dark | **owed answers** | Debt in questions you must answer truthfully when asked. Transferable. Inheritable |
| Sill | *none internally* | Allocation is computed. Money is a lossy encoding of preference |
| the Ancients | **repairs** | They fix a thing for you. There is no exchange rate |
| Osk's Flux | *everyone else's* | And they settle in instruments that resolve unexpectedly |

## Twelve governments, twelve structural flaws

Every one of these is a machine for producing the specific conflict that race is in.

| | Structure | The flaw that generates story |
|---|---|---|
| Terra | Chief archivist with accidental executive power | No succession mechanism, no removal mechanism, and eleven port-cities fighting over which dead lane gets funded |
| Sill | Modelled consensus | A model needs an objective and they have never agreed on one. Cannot lie, but may decline to publish |
| Churn | Consensus by population density | **Foreign policy is a function of geology.** A fragment has no legal category at all |
| Sarn's World | Gerontocracy by mass | Oldest = largest = slowest. Response time in decades. They have missed every war they were in |
| Bell | A guild, not a state | Power dies with the person. Losing 4% of all route knowledge per year, and the reformers are heretics |
| the Works | Task-holding authority | **Cannot sign a treaty**, only accept a job. And decisions taken by the dead execute on schedule |
| Ossa's Delta | Custodial — you speak for what you tend | Custodianship cannot be audited. And the BU 40 silence is now a generational crime |
| Kettering | Berth-rights and convoy councils | No territory → no sovereignty → **no standing**. Largest population, seated as a commercial party |
| the Ancients | An order with duties | Their purpose was completed by their own hand. Not civil war — **drift**. Units simply stop |
| Osk's Flux | Non-collapsed consensus | A fraction has not experienced the Unarriving, so no policy premised on it can pass. 74-year quorum failure |
| Ordel Deep | Aristocracy of shipwrights | Rewards not changing. Forty houses each correct four hundred times produced a dead end |
| Sable's Dark | An unlisted hierarchy | Concealment means they **cannot demonstrate strength**. Keepers vs Brokers, and the Brokers are retailing the only asset |

## The four things all twelve share

**The Whisper.** The surviving Trellis relay net. Carries voice and text instantly, everywhere;
carries no cargo, no people, and no sight. It is why twelve empires can negotiate while being
physically unable to reach each other, and why families separated in AU 0 have been talking for
seventy-four years without meeting. See Law 11 — it is the rule that makes the galaxy's politics
possible.

**The Registry.** Terra's archive, with no authority over anyone, setting the galactic standard for
mass, distance, hull class and casualty reporting because during the Shortening it was the only
institution still publishing. Every race's official name is a Registry entry, which is why each has
a Registry name and an endonym, and why the roster the player sees is a **document** rather than a
fact of nature.

**The reckoning market.** Eleven empires buying refined crystal from a living people who are made of
it. Conducted very politely. The second sentence is never said aloud.

**The Relighting.** Everyone has a Lamp programme except the Bioform Collective, who do not want the
lanes back and have said so courteously for seventy-four years. Nobody is close. Completing the tech
tree reaches the threshold, which is what Scientific Victory means, and the Galactic Wonder is a
Lamp.

## Sensory quick reference

For writing a scene fast. Full detail in each file.

| | Sound | Smell | Food |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terra | Pneumatic tubes, stamps, echoing empty freight halls | Paper dust, brass, ink, grain | Preserved everything. Fish-and-root pie eaten forty years on |
| Sill | Fans, coolant, thermal cracking like gunfire | Ozone, hot silicate | **None.** No kitchens on the planet |
| Churn | The crust cracking; several million bodies humming | Yeast, sulphur, warm bread | Fungal paste from communal troughs. Excellent. Never alone |
| Sarn's World | Nothing — but the crust *rings* for hours | Ozone and hot stone | **None** |
| Bell | Departures, constant rolling thunder | Dust, ozone, scorched ceramic | Thin, sour, eaten standing up |
| the Works | Deafening foundry, never stops | Hot metal, flux, burnt oil | **None.** No windows either |
| Ossa's Delta | Insects, water, and hulls *breathing* | Rot and blossom in the same breath | The best in the galaxy |
| Kettering | Hull subsonics, close voices, string music | Recycled air and cooking, always cooking | Best cooking, worst ingredients |
| the Ancients | Nothing. Everything runs and nothing is loud | **Nothing, deliberately** | Never observed |
| Osk's Flux | A held note slightly out of tune with itself | Ozone, scorched sugar | Portions vary. Described by distribution |
| Ordel Deep | Booms, never rings. Consonants die | Quarry dust and cooking fat | Vast, fatty, slow. Ninety minutes minimum |
| Sable's Dark | Muffled, dead acoustics, close | Fungus, cold stone, woodsmoke, spice | Superb, dark, never explained |

Three worlds have **no food culture at all** — Sill, the Works, Sarn's World — and Terran envoys
independently filed the same complaint about all three. It is the loneliest thing in the setting and
it costs nothing.

## Deliberately unresolved

Per Law 25 and the cosmology's closing section, do not fill these in:

- **The Ancients' homeworld.** Never visited, possibly nonexistent. Two hundred catalogued
  installations, all facilities, none of them anywhere anyone was born.
- **What the Ancients eat.** No kitchen has ever been catalogued.
- **What Wren saw in the lane.** She died in AU 71 with it. Never shown.
- **What is behind the quarantine.** Never named, designed, or depicted. We show a docking schedule
  with a gap in it.
- **How a Lamp worked.**
- **Whether the twelve are related.** They are not. Twelve separate ascents, each finding the roads
  already swept — which makes the Trellis stranger, because it was not built for anyone.
