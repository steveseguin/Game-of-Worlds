## The world

There is no distinction between the Crystalline Entity and the planet it lives on. This is not a
metaphor and it is the single hardest thing about them for any other species to hold in mind.

Sarn's World orbits a bright, hard star. Its crust is a silicate biosphere — a metabolising mineral
sheet, kilometres thick in places, that has been growing since before anything else in the galaxy
was alive. It draws energy from radiation and structure from uptake. It is slow beyond any other
living thing's comprehension: a Crystalline thought takes hours, a Crystalline decision takes years,
and a Crystalline lifetime has no established upper bound because none has yet ended of age.

**There are no cities. There are thicknesses.**

Where the Standing is denser and older, it is more capable — more mass, more surface, more
throughput. Those regions have names, and the names are the names of individuals, because in the
Standing a *place* and a *person* are the same noun. Elder Sarn is a terraced escarpment four
hundred kilometres long. When Ilsa Rell interviewed Sarn, she stood on it.

The metal problem follows directly. Sarn's World has ore, and it is *underneath the population*,
and extracting it means cutting through a living mineral sheet. Their metal output is 0.7 because
mining on their homeworld is a surgical and theological question, and their crystal output is 1.5
because crystal is biomass and they can grow it.

## The economy

Here is the largest unexploded device in galactic politics.

Every empire in the galaxy needs reckoning. Reckoning is refined crystal. **The Crystalline Entity
is crystal.**

The trade exists, and it functions, and it functions because the Standing sells **its own dead** —
regions that have gone inert, ceased metabolising, and become, by the Standing's own careful
definition, no longer anyone. Those are quarried, refined, and shipped, and they are the highest-grade
reckoning in the galaxy by a wide margin, and eleven empires' navigation runs on them.

The question of *what counts as inert* is the largest internal dispute in Crystalline civilisation
and has been for seventy years.

There is no currency. The Standing contracts in **duration** — agreements denominated in centuries,
with delivery schedules no other species can honour and penalty clauses that mature after the
counterparty's civilisation is expected to have changed form. They know nobody can meet these terms.
They write them anyway. Sarn has described the practice, on record, as *the only joke we have.*

**Food is radiation and mineral uptake.** They cannot be starved on any timescale a war occurs in.
They also cannot be fed, hurried, or bribed. An embargo against the Standing is a gesture.

## The politics

**Gerontocracy by mass.** Older means larger means more throughput means more voice. It is not
contested; it is arithmetic, and the Standing regards every other species' politics as an elaborate
substitute for weighing.

The flaw is the obvious one and it is catastrophic. The oldest are the largest are the *slowest*. The
Standing's institutional response time to any external event is measured in decades. They have been
formally at war three times and arrived at a position on each war after it ended. They were not
neutral. They were **still deciding.**

The live fracture, and it is a schism in the proper sense:

**The Yielding.** Sell the inert. Engage. Trade reckoning, accept envoys, participate. Their argument
is practical and generous: the galaxy is blind and frightened and we have what it needs, and refusing
would be a cruelty.

**The Whole.** Nothing of us leaves. Their argument is that seventy years of the Yielding's generosity
has produced a galaxy that speaks of the Standing as a *deposit* — that eleven empires have quarterly
reports with a line item that is a person — and that no amount of politeness has changed this or ever
will.

**The Whole are winning.** Slowly, which for them is the only speed, and the projection is that
somewhere in the next century the reckoning market loses its highest-grade supplier. Every admiralty
in the galaxy knows this and none of them can do anything about it, because there is no Crystalline
faction to support and no election to influence — there is only a very old thing gradually being
persuaded.

## Culture, faith, and habit

The Standing has the closest thing to a genuine religion in the setting, and its central doctrine is
**correct**, which is a rare and wonderful thing to do with a faith.

**The Setting Down.** Sarn's testimony has become scripture. The belief is that in AU 0 the galaxy was
not broken but *put down* — deliberately, carefully, by something with manners — and that the
appropriate response is patience, and that the empires rushing to relight the lanes are children
trying to pick up a thing that was set down for a reason.

They have been saying this for seventy-four years to anyone who asks. Nobody can act on it, because
what they are offering is a feeling, and there is no box on the intake form for a feeling. *An
accident has no manners* is a liturgical phrase now. It is inscribed — grown, rather — at eleven
thicknesses.

**Duration as courtesy.** To a Crystalline, arriving at a decision quickly is a discourtesy: it
implies the matter was not worth consideration. Their diplomatic protocol requires a delay
proportional to the importance of the question, which means the Standing's most urgent
communications are its slowest, and at least one war has started because an empire read a
seventeen-year silence as contempt.

**They do not have art.** They have *records* — structural changes made deliberately and permanently
in their own bodies, to be read by whatever is standing there in ten thousand years. The Standing
writes in itself. This is why they find the Void Walkers' refusal to write anything down not merely
foolish but obscene.

## The senses

**Sound.** Sarn's World is the quietest inhabited place in the galaxy and the most resonant. There is
no wind worth mentioning, no water, no animals. But the crust *rings*: strike it and the tone holds
for hours, and a large enough impact anywhere on the planet is audible everywhere on it for days. The
Standing's own communication is partly conducted this way, and standing on the surface during a
conversation is a physical experience.

**Smell.** Ozone and hot stone. Nothing else. There is no organic decay anywhere on the planet
because there is nothing organic.

**Sight.** Translucent. Everything is translucent — terraces, fins, sheets and escarpments of
grey-green and amber mineral, kilometres across, with light travelling *through* the landscape and
emerging somewhere else. At dusk the whole horizon glows from within. There is no way to tell where
the geology stops and the person begins, and there is no answer to that question either.

**Food.** None. No kitchens, no fires, no markets, no smell of a meal on the entire world. Combined
with the silence and the absence of anything soft, envoys report Sarn's World as *beautiful and
unbearable* — the phrase appears in three separate Registry postings — and rotations are limited to
eight months.

## History that still bites

- **Before records.** The Standing is older than every other civilisation's oldest document, and it
  remembers the Trellis being *already there*, which makes it the only witness to the fact that
  nobody built it in living memory. It cannot say more than that. It was not paying attention; it had
  no reason to.
- **BU 300.** First reckoning sale. The Yielding's founding act, taken for good reasons, and the
  Whole have been arguing about it for six hundred years.
- **AU 0.** The Standing feels the shutdown, everywhere, at once. It does not hurt. See
  `10-the-long-file/04-crystalline.md`.
- **AU 34 and AU 44.** Ilsa Rell interviews Sarn, twice, ten years apart. Sarn: *you are the first
  one to ask me twice.*

## Their place in the Trellis story

They are made of the same substance as the Lamps, and they felt the shutdown from the inside of the
material, and their testimony is the reason anyone believes it was deliberate. They cannot prove it.
It is the least verifiable and most consequential claim in the file.

They are also the reason the Trellis question is an *economic* question and not merely a historical
one. Every probe launched, every fleet moved, every agent paid, in eleven empires, is burning a
refined fragment of the same material as the thing that used to keep everyone safe — and a person
sold it to you.

**Weapons cap 2 and missiles locked** because a Crystalline weapon is not manufactured — it is part of
the body, formidable and unimprovable. They have never had the concept of an armament separate from a
self, which is also why attack is ×1.2 while the research ladder is capped: innately potent,
technologically frozen. **No Carrier**: a hull that carries other hulls is, to the Standing, an
incoherent object. Per Law 20: silicate life, no phasing, no teleportation, no communication at
range.
