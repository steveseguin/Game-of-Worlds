# Visual Reference Prompt Ledger

Generation mode: **OpenAI built-in image generation**.

This ledger records the final prompt set in normalized form. The shared direction below was applied
to every new board, followed by the board-specific content. Boards 09 and 11 received one corrective
edit each; those edit instructions are recorded after their original specifications.

## Shared direction

Create an original, production-quality visual-reference board for the science-fiction strategy game
*Game of Worlds*. Use a fixed-camera, painterly 2D/pre-rendered command-station aesthetic with subtle
bitmap texture and modern polish: charcoal black, iron, oxidized red, ochre, bone, and restrained
crystal cyan; hard practical lighting, deep shadow, screen glow, worn surfaces, visible repair, and
clear silhouettes. Avoid glossy generic concept art, franchise resemblance, low-poly 3D, rounded
mobile-game UI, readable text, labels, logos, watermarks, and decorative typography. Separate panels
with narrow dark metal gutters. Maintain equal visual importance across panels.

## Board specifications

### 01 — races: Terran to Crystalline

Four equal panels:

1. Terran: a tired, competent human command officer in a repaired dark uniform at a paper-cluttered
   console; tape, pencil notes, serial plates, no heroic armour.
2. Silicon Collective: no humanoid robot and no face; a distributed computational chamber of ceramic
   heat sinks, cable logic, precise white-blue indicators, and machinery whose intelligence is
   inferred through operation.
3. Zephyr Swarm: ochre/rust population density, many small bodies and occupied structures read as a
   single moving social mass; abundance and momentum rather than an insect close-up.
4. Crystalline: a massive faceted mineral person or civic presence, internally lit cyan/amber,
   geological stress lines and refracted light, with no clothing pasted onto human anatomy.

### 02 — races: Void Walkers to Star Nomads

Four equal panels:

1. Void Walkers: dust, bone, layered written memory, worn route records, and one restrained saturated
   route mark; people defined by recitation and continuity.
2. Mechanicus: an active industrial shift with gantries, repeated maintenance tools, conveyors, and
   worker-machines; no singular robot hero and no theatrical ornament.
3. Bioform: living infrastructure in which organism, room, vessel, and cultivated tissue share one
   material language; wet translucence without horror-gore cliché.
4. Star Nomads: modular habitat/ship interior, route cords, repaired inherited objects, schedules and
   practical communal life; patched and mobile rather than sleek.

### 03 — races: Ancients to Shadow Realm

Four equal panels:

1. Ancients: show only an impossible installation, relay geometry, and an absence where an operator
   should be. Never depict an Ancient body, face, statue, or costume.
2. Quantum: a precise figure or apparatus appearing in mutually exclusive positions, doubled edges
   and conditional states, controlled spectral colour, scientific rather than supernatural.
3. Titan Lords: infrastructure-scale anatomy and machinery, a vast hand/joint/chamber that makes
   ordinary ships look tiny; weight, age, and pressure.
4. Shadow Realm: warm darkness, overlapping short-sightline rooms, market traces, concealed
   inhabitants, and deliberate negative space; no official emblem and nothing easily countable.

### 04 — Terran characters

Four equal portrait/environment panels:

1. Chart-Warden Rell: middle-aged, androgynous, exhausted but exacting, worn command uniform, paper
   file and pencil at a dim console; not glamorous and not posed as an action hero.
2. Registrar Vance: practical institutional head, disciplined posture, files and a tactical display,
   visibly responsible for people rather than decorated for spectacle.
3. Sweep-Admiral Ito: scientist-strategist among instruments and annotated physical records;
   curiosity held inside rigorous procedure.
4. Navigator Sesse: alert pilot in a repaired flight rig, compact cockpit, route marks and hand-used
   controls; capable and human-scale.

### 05 — non-Terran characters

Four equal portrait/environment panels:

1. Liaison Fourth: Silicon presence represented through an interface chamber, heat sinks, precise
   indicators, and a deliberately prepared human contact surface; no robot face.
2. Keth: Star Nomad trace-broker at a market table, layered practical textiles, inherited route
   objects, warm hospitality, and a chart treated as merchandise and memorial at once.
3. Analyst Nine: Silicon analyst represented by a precise instrument and heat-management interface;
   conditional readouts and disciplined observation, no robot face.
4. The Emissary: Ancient contact represented only by a lit installation, impossible geometry, and
   the pressure of an unseen presence. Never show its body.

### 06 — standard ship classes

Nine equal orthographic/action-reference panels in a 3×3 grid, with a coherent repaired Terran/neutral
baseline fleet language and visibly different silhouettes:

1. Frigate: compact escort.
2. Destroyer: forward-weighted attack hull.
3. Scout: small sensor-rich long-range craft.
4. Cruiser: balanced line ship.
5. Battleship: armoured broadside mass.
6. Colony Ship: capacious civilian/industrial vessel.
7. Dreadnought: enormous slow weapons platform.
8. Intruder: narrow, stealth-oriented infiltration craft.
9. Carrier: hangar-dominant fleet support hull with small craft scale cues.

Do not add race insignia or labels. This is a role/silhouette reference, not a complete race-specific
fleet set.

### 07 — homeworlds

Twelve equal environmental panels in a 4×3 grid:

1. Terra: repaired old cities beneath command infrastructure.
2. Sill: Silicon heat-management landscape and computational geology.
3. Churn: dense ochre Zephyr habitation in constant motion.
4. Sarn's World: faceted mineral terrain and internally lit Crystalline settlements.
5. Bell: Void Walker dust/bone settlements layered with records and routes.
6. Works: Mechanicus world-factory whose horizon is maintenance and production.
7. Ossa's Delta: Bioform wetland/city grown as one ecology.
8. Kettering: Star Nomad modular anchorage and inherited mobile habitats.
9. Ancient homeworld unknown: show an abandoned installation, not a claimed planet and not a body.
10. Osk's Flux: Quantum environment with conditional geography and doubled boundaries.
11. Ordel Deep: Titan-scale pressure architecture and immense inhabited depth.
12. Sable's Dark: warm-dark, short-sightline Shadow settlement that resists orbital counting.

### 08 — battles and hazards

Four equal cinematic tactical panels:

1. A fleet making a blind crossing through a dense asteroid shoal, individual ships being lost while
   others push toward a route that can become safe after sweeping/control.
2. A fleet arriving at a collapsar/black hole and being annihilated with no escape, shown as severe
   red/orange sensor distortion rather than colourful spectacle.
3. A Zephyr saturation attack: many modest craft, dense motion, and one meaningful absence/gap in the
   population pattern.
4. Missiles passing around or through the assumptions of a Crystalline shield, with heavy faceted
   defenders and clear kinetic threat.

### 09 — crystal, relics, and exploration

Six equal object/vehicle panels in a 3×2 grid:

1. Precision-cut reckoning crystal, cyan, instrument-grade, held in a practical mount.
2. Raw crystal in dark mineral matrix, irregular and materially related to the refined form.
3. Handheld relic: seamless, unreadable, non-decorative, and clearly not a magical rune object.
4. Structural relic: seamless continuous material embedded in a world site, with no panel joints,
   assembly seams, doors, or obvious maker's language.
5. Long-range probe: expendable, sensor-rich, compact, and visibly built to report until telemetry
   ends.
6. Universal relic lifter: dedicated transport cradle usable by every race, clearly distinct from a
   colony ship and designed to move one dangerous/precious physical object.

Corrective edit applied: replace only the handheld and structural relic imagery so both read as
continuous seamless material with no seams, joints, panel lines, hinges, doors, assembly gaps, runes,
or readable maker marks. Preserve all other panels, composition, palette, lighting, and style.

### 10 — weapons

Six equal technical/action panels in a 3×2 grid:

1. Laser weapon: disciplined coherent beam and practical emitter.
2. Plasma projector: contained luminous mass with dangerous thermal spill.
3. Basic rocket: rugged chemical munition and simple launcher.
4. Hyper-V missile: high-velocity precision weapon with severe acceleration cues.
5. Antimatter warhead: heavily contained device whose danger is expressed by engineering restraint,
   not fantasy ornament.
6. Crystalline Ringer: race-specific resonance weapon acting through faceted mineral structures,
   visualized as pressure/refraction rather than magic.

### 11 — Wonders

Twelve equal monumental panels in a 4×3 grid. These are **design references**, not shipped content:

1. Terran — Concord Lamp: an immense repaired beacon assembled through documentation and common work.
2. Silicon — Solved Sky: a computational observatory resolving the dark through total calculation.
3. Zephyr — Ten Thousand Mouths: overwhelming inhabited expansion and population infrastructure.
4. Crystalline — First Thickness: a vast non-humanoid living geological formation becoming a Lamp;
   no statue, face, torso, or human pose.
5. Void Walkers — Whole Recitation: a monumental archive/recitation chamber of layered records and
   routes, not a generic city skyline.
6. Mechanicus — Endless Yard: production continuing beyond the horizon without completion theatre.
7. Bioform — Mother Grove: a world-scale living ecology that makes the road unnecessary.
8. Star Nomads — Reconciled Schedule: routes, names, modules, and timings finally brought into one
   practical moving system.
9. Ancients — Closing: an impossible installation sealing the old error, with no Ancient body.
10. Quantum — Unresolved Lane: a route that conditionally declines to have needed ordinary travel.
11. Titan Lords — Weight of Ages: mass and inhabited scale so great that the dark becomes weather.
12. Shadow Realm — Assembled Frame: distributed recovered fragments forming a readable sequence or
    archive, deliberately without a construction site.

Corrective edit applied: replace only First Thickness and Whole Recitation. First Thickness became a
colossal non-humanoid geological/living crystal formation; Whole Recitation became an unmistakable
record chamber with dust, bone, layered tablets, route cords, and one restrained saturated route
mark. Preserve the other ten panels.

## Negative constraints retained across the set

- No invented readable alien script.
- No typography-dependent identification.
- No franchise uniforms, famous silhouettes, or recognizable logos.
- No Ancient bodies.
- No humanoid Silicon mascot.
- No generic purple-eye symbol for the Shadow Realm.
- No green insect shorthand for the Zephyr.
- No magical seams or runes on relics.
- No claim that Wonders or relic mechanics are already available in the game.
