# Account 12 — Shadow Realm

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | The Third Question |
| **The work** | Senior Keeper. Has extended the offer twice |
| **Recorded** | AU 74 |
| **About** | The commander who accepted, and did not believe it |

---

You have found the right room and nobody told you where it was, and I would very much like to know how, and
I am not going to ask, because you would tell me and then I would owe you.

Sit. There is food. There is always food; it is the only thing about us that is not complicated.

You want the offer.

Before an engagement we extend hospitality to the opposing commander. Genuinely, with terms, and it is
honoured if accepted. Three have accepted in seventy-four years. I hosted the second.

Sweep-Admiral of the Silicon Collective — a unit, you understand, not a person as you would mean it. Sector
7-B, in AU 58. It came alone, as the terms required. It stayed four days.

We fed it, which took some arranging, since it does not eat; we produced power to specification and it was
courteous about the effort. We gave it the good rooms. We talked for four days about nothing whatsoever —
about the shoals, about your Registry's classification schedule, about a piece of Nomad music it had modelled
and could not account for enjoying. It was extremely good company. I have not had better.

On the fourth day I returned it to its fleet, unharmed, with its escort, and I corrected its intelligence
assessment of our strength.

**In the wrong direction.**

Downward. I told it we were weaker than it believed. Which was true — we are weaker than everyone believes,
that is the entire condition of my people — and I told it so, and it thanked me, and it left, and it filed a
report.

The report is in your cabinet. You have read it. It says: *hospitality extended, accepted, four days, no
intelligence obtained. Assessment: a trap of undetermined design. Recommend the offer never be accepted
again.*

Warden, it was not a trap.

There was no second layer. There was no message in the seating or the food or the four days of nothing
whatsoever. I gave a guest the good rooms and the truth and I sent it home, and it wrote *a trap of
undetermined design*, and the Collective has declined the offer eleven times since, and I do not blame them
and I would write the same report.

That is the cost of being us and it is not a complaint. We have made ourselves unbelievable on purpose over
six hundred years. It has kept us alive, it has kept us uncounted, it is the only thing we have, and the
price is that **on the one occasion we are entirely sincere, there is no instrument in the galaxy that can
detect it.**

I have extended the offer twice. It was accepted once.

You will be wondering whether the Brokers know I have told you this, and whether I have told you because
Sable gave a fragment away for nothing and I have found that difficult to answer, and whether the Keepers
are still losing.

What a very good set of questions.

Have some more of the dark bread. It is the last of the season and you will not get it anywhere else, and
you have had almost nothing.

---

## Notes

**Why it's a Shadow.** Opens by noticing something and declining to ask about it, because asking would
create a debt. Answers the question asked and then, at the end, restates Rell's three real questions with
total accuracy and answers none of them — which is the house style, and it is delivered as hospitality
rather than evasion. And the last thing said is about bread.

**The reveal, and it is the whole race.** The offer is real. It has always been real. It is the one Unlisted
practice that is not transactional, and it is the thing they are least believed about, and the report in
Terra's cabinet reads *a trap of undetermined design* — filed by a Collective unit that had four genuinely
happy days and could not model an explanation.

*"On the one occasion we are entirely sincere, there is no instrument in the galaxy that can detect it."*
Six hundred years of deliberate unbelievability, and the bill arrives as a filing.

**The Silicon detail that makes it land.** They had to produce *power to specification* for a guest that does
not eat, and the unit was courteous about the effort, and it enjoyed a piece of Nomad music it could not
account for enjoying. Pair that with Account 2: a Collective unit acquiring an emergent bias it cannot find
the mechanism for. **Two independent accounts of the Solved feeling something and filing it as an anomaly.**

**The three unanswered questions are canon-accurate.** The Brokers *are* still winning
(`../14-peoples/12-shadow-realm.md`), Sable *did* give a fragment away for nothing, and the Third Question is
a title held by three individuals since AU 0 with no outside power noticing the transitions. Every one of
Rell's questions is the right question. None gets an answer. That is the character.

**VO note.** Delighted, warm, hospitable, never sinister. If the performer plays menace the character
disappears. *"Warden, it was not a trap"* is the only line in the whole account delivered plainly, and it
should sound like relief.
