# Account 3 — Zephyr Swarm

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Unnamed. A rejoined fragment, Vantage basin |
| **The work** | Sweeping. It was in a saturation element that was cut off for four years |
| **Recorded** | AU 74 |
| **About** | It came home. It cannot remember being alone |

---

We are asked about the four years. We can answer. We have the four years. They are in we.

We went out in sixty-eight, eleven thousand of we, to take a shoal at 9-B. We took it. It is swept, it is
ours, it is a good crossing now and there are things growing on it. That is the answer to what you asked
and we do not think it is what you meant.

The cut happened on the way back. We do not know the cause; the cause is not in we. Four hundred of we
were on the far side of the cut and eleven thousand were on this side, and the four hundred were out
there for four years, and then a nest came within range and the four hundred came home.

We are the four hundred. Or we are the eleven thousand. We are we; the distinction you are drawing does
not hold in our language and we are trying to hold it for you because you asked politely.

Here is the difficulty.

We have the four years. We have every observation. We can tell you the drift of 9-B's outer margin to
three decimals and the failure rate of a hull left unrepaired for forty-one months and where there is
water in that region, which nobody else knows and which we are happy to give you.

**We have the fact and not the having of it.**

Four hundred of we were alone. We know this the way you know a date in a book. When we came home the four
hundred went into eleven thousand and then into the whole of we, and what four hundred experienced across
four years is now distributed across a great many, and each of we holds a portion of it, and the portion
is too small to be a thing that happened *to* anybody.

So: were we frightened? We have no access. Something in the record suggests a condition we do not have a
word for and cannot locate. It is like being told that a room you are standing in was cold last year.

We would like to say something and we are not certain we are permitted to, because it is about the other
one.

We know there is another. Out past the frontier. Eleven hundred, cut in the nine days, seventy years and
it has not come home. Some of we have said it is not real. Some of we have said it is not anything, which
is the correct construction. We have been out there and we have come back and we think we know why it
does not come.

If it comes home it will be us. It will have the fact and not the having of it. And whatever it has been
holding for seventy years will be portioned out until nobody is carrying it.

That is not a loss to we. We want to be clear. It is not a loss to we at all.

We think it may be a loss.

We do not have anywhere to put that sentence, so we have given it to you. You have a cabinet.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Zephyr.** Plural throughout, no singular pronoun available, and the whole account is a species
attempting to describe an experience its grammar forbids — cheerfully, cooperatively, and failing. It
opens by answering the operational question (the shoal is swept, things are growing on it) because that
genuinely is what it thinks was asked.

**The counterpoint to Series 9.** The Remainder refused to go home because rejoining erases the memory.
Nobody believed that mattered. **This is the proof.** A fragment that did rejoin, holding four years of
data and no experience of it, working out unaided why the other one stays out — and then, in the last
three lines, arriving at the edge of a moral intuition it has no vocabulary for and handing it to a Terran
because Terrans have somewhere to put things.

*"We do not have anywhere to put that sentence, so we have given it to you. You have a cabinet."* That is
the entire justification for the Registry, offered by the least literate civilisation in the galaxy.

**VO note.** Same rules as the Remainder: one performer, flat and gentle, **no audio treatment of any
kind.** The final three lines must not be played as a realisation. It is thinking aloud and running out of
language, which is a different thing and much worse.

**Art beat.** 9-B. A swept shoal with something growing on it, and four hundred hulls parked in the margin
for four years, unrepaired, in neat rows.
