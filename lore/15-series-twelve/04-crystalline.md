# Account 4 — Crystalline Entity

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Elder Tessen of the Standing |
| **The work** | Determinations of inertness. Eleven in ninety years |
| **Recorded** | AU 73 |
| **About** | The one from AU 31, which is now in eleven fleets |

---

You have come to ask the Whole's leader about the trade she wants stopped. That is a good question and it
is not the one you should be asking, so I will answer the one you should.

I have made eleven determinations.

You know what a determination is; you have read Sarn. A region ceases to metabolise. It must be decided
whether it has gone inert — whether it is no longer anyone — and the deciding is done slowly by the largest
elders nearby, and it cannot be reversed, and every load of reckoning in your galaxy rests on one of them.

I have made eleven. Ten of them I would make again this afternoon.

The other was in AU 31.

It was the Ashgate margin. Four hundred and nine square kilometres, thin, old, and it had gone quiet
across about eleven years. Quiet is not the same as inert. Quiet means no throughput we can read. We waited
nine years past the last reading, which is the standing interval, and then three of us weighed it and we
determined it inert, and it was quarried.

I want you to understand the pressure and I do not want you to think I am offering it as an excuse.

AU 31 was the eleventh year of the Sweeping. Your Registry had eight empires begging for reckoning; there
was not enough refined crystal in the galaxy to probe a tenth of what needed probing, and fleets were
crossing blind that did not have to cross blind, and dying, in numbers your grandmother's file contains.
Ashgate was four hundred thousand tonnes of the highest-grade reckoning anyone has ever refined. It is in
eleven fleets. It has bought — I have had the arithmetic done, by the Solved, who did not ask why — it has
bought somewhere on the order of two thousand probes and eleven thousand fewer dead than there would
otherwise have been.

Nine years ago I felt something in the Ashgate quarry face.

Not a reading. There is no reading; I have had it measured by every instrument the Collective will lend us
and there is nothing. A feeling, in the material, which is the only sense I have that you would accept as
a sense, and which I have spent seventy years telling your empires is worth listening to.

I do not know whether Ashgate was inert.

I cannot find out. It is in eleven fleets. It has been consumed — burned, spent, gone, in the drives and
probes and nav-cores of eight empires over forty-two years, and there is no piece of it left large enough
to weigh, and there never will be.

So. You may write down that the leader of the Whole wants the trade stopped, and you may write down why,
and everyone will read it as doctrine.

It is not doctrine. It is one determination, made carefully, under pressure, for good reasons, by three
elders who were not certain, and I was the largest of the three, and the weight was mine.

Sarn asks me every few years to withdraw the phrase *the deliberate murder of meaning.* I will not.

Go and ask me again in forty years. I will still be here. I will not have found out.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Crystalline.** Durations rather than dates, refuses the interviewer's framing in the first
sentence, measures the cost in both directions with total honesty, and closes by inviting a return visit in
forty years. It never once claims certainty and never once asks for sympathy.

**The structural move.** The leader of the faction that wants the reckoning trade stopped is required, by
mass, to be one of the elders who authorises it — and her position is not ideology, it is one specific
determination she cannot verify. **This is how you write a faction leader**: give them a reason that is
private, particular, and unfalsifiable, and let the doctrine be the thing other people mistake it for.

**And the honest counterweight.** Ashgate bought two thousand probes and eleven thousand fewer dead. Tessen
says so herself, with the arithmetic done by the Collective. The trade she wants stopped **worked**, at a
scale nobody disputes, and she still cannot find out whether she sold a person. There is no clean answer
available and the account does not pretend there is one.

**VO note.** Slow, warm, unhurried, entirely without self-pity. The single hardest line to place is *"I do
not know whether Ashgate was inert"* — it must be delivered as a fact she has finished being frightened of.

**Art beat.** The Ashgate quarry face: four hundred square kilometres of cut translucent mineral, terraced,
worked out, empty, and light still travelling through it.
