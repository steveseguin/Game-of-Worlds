# Account 1 — Terran Empire

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Deputy Warden Halloway, Ordel Gate |
| **The work** | Writes the notifications. Nine hundred and forty of them |
| **Recorded** | AU 72 |
| **About** | The one she could not write, because the form had no box for it |

---

I have written nine hundred and forty. I can tell you the form number, I can tell you which boxes take
the manifest reference and which take the sector, and I can tell you that the third paragraph is
pre-printed and that this is correct, because the third paragraph is the part nobody should have to
compose twice.

You want the one from AU 69.

Eleven hulls, sector 4-F. It had been swept. It had been swept eight years earlier by a Nomad-Terran
joint element, certified, filed, and it had been crossed *forty-one times* since without a loss. Forty-one.
I have the tally because I keep the tally, and it is the tally that makes it my business rather than the
Admiralty's.

I certified the trace. My hand, my grade number, cabinet nine, drawer four.

The shoal had moved.

That is all. It drifted. Belts do that; it is in the survey literature and it is in the training and every
Chart-Warden knows it and none of us knows how to *use* the knowing, because we cannot re-survey forty
thousand crossings a season. So a certified corridor that had been safe for eight years and forty-one
transits was not safe on the forty-second, and eleven hulls arrived into gravel at closing speed, and I
had to write to fourteen families.

And I could not do it.

Not — I want to be exact with you, because you will file this — not from feeling. I sat down and I could
not complete the form. Section four is *Cause*, and it is a coded field, and the codes are: hazard,
enemy action, structural, navigational error, other. Navigational error means somebody made a mistake.
Hazard means the sector was known to be dangerous.

Neither of those is true. The chart was *right when we filed it*. There is no code for that. I checked the
schedule of amendments back to AU 11 and there has never been one.

So I wrote it in the margin. Longhand. *Certified corridor, drift, no error by any party, chart accurate
at date of filing.* Twenty-two words, outside the boxes, on all fourteen.

I was found irregular. It is on my record and it will be on my record when I retire, and my grade
progression stopped, and I understand the finding and I do not dispute it. A margin is not a field. If
every Warden writes in the margin the series stops being searchable and then in ninety years nobody can
count anything, and counting is the whole of what we are for.

But those fourteen families were going to read *navigational error* about people who did not make one.

I would do it again. I have said that in a hearing, which is why my grade progression stopped.

I have written nine hundred and forty. Section four is still a coded field. I have submitted a proposed
amendment three times.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Terran.** Her defence is procedural and her heresy is a margin. Twenty-two words in longhand
ended a career, and she is not bitter and she is not martyred — she agrees with the finding and she broke
the rule anyway, which is the exact opposite of Clerk Ames in Series 9. Two Terrans, the same institution,
sixty years apart: one filed correctly and it ended the galaxy, one filed irregularly and it ended her
promotion. **Read the two accounts together.**

**The mechanic underneath.** Traces decay — Law 10 in `../11-laws-of-the-world.md`. This is the first time
the fiction has shown what that costs, and it shows it as a *coded field*, which is the most Terran
possible framing of a fleet dying.

**VO note.** Brisk, technical, unemotional, and she gives the cabinet and drawer number unprompted. The
only place to let anything through is *"and I could not do it"* — and then she immediately corrects the
listener's assumption about why, which is the whole character.

**Art beat.** The form. Section four, five printed codes, and twenty-two words of handwriting running off
the edge of the box into white space. That single image is the Terran Empire.
