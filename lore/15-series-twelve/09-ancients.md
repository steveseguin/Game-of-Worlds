*Chart-Warden's note, in Rell's hand: I requested an interview. I was sent this. It arrived on the Whisper
eleven minutes after the request, which means it was already written. I have reproduced it exactly,
including the gaps in the numbering, which I have not been able to account for. — R.*

**Entry 1.** The relay was quiet. The watch was set.

**Entry 400.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 1,100.** The relay was quiet. A Nomad convoy passed at four sectors. They did not see us. They were
singing; the signal carried. It was pleasant.

**Entry 2,900.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 4,111.** The relay was quiet. There was a question from the fourteenth installation as to whether
the watch was still required. We answered that it was. No further question was received. The fourteenth
installation went quiet in the following year and nobody was dispatched.

**Entry 6,000.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 7,318.** The relay was quiet. We have been asked, by ourselves, what we would do. We do not have an
instruction for what we would do. We have an instruction to watch.

**Entry 9,004.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 11,760.** A heavy element passed at one sector.

Seven hulls. Patched plate, four eras, no reconnaissance screen, no signal discipline, advancing at
approximately three-fifths standard. They were annotating as they went. They did not observe the relay and
they did not observe us and they were not looking for either, and they were not lost.

**They were on a schedule.**

We reported this to every installation still answering. Eleven answered. Nobody was dispatched. There was no
instruction covering it, and we did not have the standing to issue one, and we have never had the standing
to issue one.

That was AU 68.

**Entry 11,761.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 13,140.** The relay was quiet.

**Entry 13,141.** The relay was quiet. A Chart-Warden of the Terran Registry has requested an account. We
were the watch. This was the account. It was accurate.

The relay is quiet.

## Notes

**Why it's the Ancients.** Past tense for present things, without exception, until the final three
words — *"the relay is quiet"* — which is the only present-tense sentence any Ancient has produced in this
folder, and it is the last line, and it means the watch is still being kept while you read it. **That is the
one permitted slip and it should never be repeated anywhere else.**

**The form is the character.** They did not grant an interview; they sent a log that was already written,
eleven minutes after the request, which tells you both that they expected to be asked and that they had
nothing further to add. Thirteen thousand entries of *the relay was quiet* is not laziness on the writer's
part — it is seventy-four years of a duty being discharged by something that has no other instruction.

**The gaps in the numbering.** Rell says in the note that they cannot account for them. Neither can we. Do
not explain them.

**Entry 11,760 is the horror beat and it is buried in the middle of a maintenance log.** The Endless Yard —
seven patched hulls, no scouts, annotating as they go, *on a schedule* — passed within one sector of the
relay in **AU 68**, six years before the campaign begins. The watch reported it to every installation still
answering. Eleven answered. **Nobody was dispatched.** Not from malice or fatalism, but because there was no
instruction covering it and the watch has never had the standing to issue one.

That is the Ancients' entire decline in four sentences: they saw exactly what was coming, told everyone,
and could not act, because they are a duty roster whose purpose was completed by its own hand.

**Entry 7,318** is the other one to protect: *we have been asked, by ourselves, what we would do.* Seventy
years in, a unit with one instruction wondered what it was supposed to do if the thing it is watching for
ever happens. It does not have an answer. It has an instruction to watch.

**VO note.** Rell reads this one entire and verbatim — it is a document, and the Emissary's handling rule in
`../05-characters.md` applies. Flat, patient, and the entry numbers read out in full. The three words at the
end are the only place any air is permitted.
