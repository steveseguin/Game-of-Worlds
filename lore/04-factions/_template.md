## Doctrine

*Their answer to the dark, in three or four paragraphs. Must be **rigorous and wrong**, never
stupid. Every faction believes something defensible; the drama is that all twelve were right
under the Trellis and none of them are right now. State the reasoning as they would state it,
then let the reader notice the flaw themselves.*

---

## History

*Four beats, and no more than a paragraph each. Resist the urge to write centuries.*

**Under the Concord.** *What were they, when the lanes were lit? This is where the
over-specialisation happened, and it should explain the capped branch.*

**The Unarriving.** *What did nine days of ships not arriving do to them specifically?*

**The Shortening.** *Famine and collapse. Who did they become to survive it?*

**Now.** *Their present position, in concrete terms. Territory, fleet, standing, and the
thing they are currently doing that will cause the campaign's trouble.*

---

## The refusal, and what it costs

*One heading per locked or capped branch, each stated as a belief. Then, plainly, what the
weakness costs them on the battlefield and in the story. Do not apologise for the weakness.
Never write "but they compensate with..." — the weakness is the point.*

---

## Government and cast

*How authority works — and prefer something structurally strange to a king with a name. Then
two or three seed characters, each with: a name (two syllables, no apostrophes, easy to say
aloud), a position, a want, and a private problem. One of them should be someone the player
can talk to.*

---

## Voice

*Their grammatical signature — the thing that makes a line identifiable with the speaker tag
removed. Tense, person, sentence length, what they never say.*

**Rules:** *three to five, specific and enforceable.*

**Samples:** *four or five lines across registers — event feed, briefing, leader in council,
someone junior and off duty. Every race must be able to say the same sentence about the same
event and sound nothing alike. The test sentence is: "we went to sector 6-B and lost four
hulls to a shoal."*

---

## How they fight

*Four bullets, each tied to a real number from `races.js` or `RACE_ACCESS`. Fleet composition,
tactical preference, what beats them, what they beat. Then the one asymmetry a player will
remember after playing against them.*

## Art notes *(for Pass 6)*

*Silhouette, palette, materials, insignia, and interior. Aim for **one defining visual idea**
an artist could build the whole race from — the Terran clipboard, the Mechanicus quilt. If
there isn't one, keep looking; a palette is not an idea.*

## Three story hooks

*Numbered. Each one a situation with a decision in it, not a piece of backstory. Each should
be usable as a campaign mission, a diplomatic event, or a discovery. At least one must connect
to the Trellis plot.*

## Canon watch-list
