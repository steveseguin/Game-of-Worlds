# The Not-Yet

Pure lore. Place and economy are in `../12-civilisations/10-quantum.md`.

---

## Aesthetic

**Form language: refusing to commit.** Shells, interference patterns, curves that do not close. **Nothing
has a defined corner.** There is no straight line anywhere in a Not-Yet settlement and no repeated element
anywhere in a Not-Yet structure, because repetition is a commitment and symmetry is a claim.

**Edges do not resolve.** A wall viewed steadily appears to have a boundary, and looked at again does not,
and two photographs of it differ. This is not a rendering trick in-world — it is the high-flux environment
doing what it does at building scale. Other species report headaches and mild nausea within a few hours,
and Registry postings are capped at four months, the shortest of any station.

**Palette:** iridescent and shifting. Nobody has ever produced a usable colour reference for a Not-Yet
city, and two competent surveys eleven years apart returned population figures differing by a factor of
three, and both were correct.

**Insignia: they do not sign things.** Not concealment — a signature asserts that a specific thing was
made by a specific maker, and the Not-Yet regard that as an overstatement.

**People:** they eat, and they do not agree on what. Portions vary. A Not-Yet meal has no fixed menu; the
dish is described by its distribution, and it is genuinely excellent about seventy per cent of the time,
and guests are warned in advance because it would be discourteous not to.

## Personality

Brisk, exasperating, and completely scrupulous.

The mistake everybody makes about the Not-Yet is reading them as dreamy or evasive. They are neither. They
are the most careful people in the galaxy about not overstating, and their grammar — conditional and
subjunctive throughout — is doing real work about states they consider unresolved. Every sentence is a
good-faith attempt at accuracy in a tense the listener cannot use.

They are also visibly irritated most of the time. *Please do not sigh. You have sighed. We have been sighed
at by eleven empires and it does not improve the physics.*

**Their humour** is bone-dry and mostly at their own expense, and the best of it is the throwaway: *We
would rather not be present for it. We may not have been.*

**Their virtue:** they will not assert what they do not hold. In a galaxy where every empire's casualty
figure is politically useful to whoever published it, the Not-Yet are the only people who have never
published a number they could not defend.

**Their vice:** **they cannot commit to anything.** It is not cowardice and it is not indecision; it is
constitutional. And it means that on the one question that matters — whether the Unarriving happened —
they have been unable to act for seventy-four years, and eleven empires read that as evasion, and it is in
fact the most scrupulous behaviour in the galaxy.

**The contradiction, stated properly, because "they are indecisive" is a trait and not a character:** a
people who refuse every commitment have built their entire moral life around the one commitment each of
them *must* make and can never revoke.

At majority every Not-Yet fixes exactly one certainty, publicly and permanently, and it may never be
amended. So the least committed civilisation in the galaxy is composed, individually, of people each
carrying a single unamendable vow chosen at nineteen — and because they refuse to commemorate, that vow is
the **only** thing any of them will ever be certain about, including about the people they love. Ysse
chose Aven, Aven died, and Ysse is the one Not-Yet in existence obliged to hold a dead person as a
resolved state while everybody around her says *Aven may be* (`../15-series-twelve/10-quantum.md`).

**Their flexibility is purchased with one irrevocable act each, and the more scrupulously a Not-Yet lives,
the more that single fixed point costs them.** That is not a limitation. That is a people whose virtue and
whose wound are the same rite.

## Leadership and structure — AU 74

**No decision is final.** Governance is non-collapsed consensus: a policy exists in several states at once
and is resolved only by being acted upon, at which point the other states are not overturned so much as
**not selected**. They consider this obviously superior to voting, on the grounds that voting throws
information away.

It works, slowly, for ordinary matters.

**The Deferred Quorum.** On one matter it has produced a seventy-four-year constitutional failure. A
meaningful fraction of the Not-Yet has not experienced the Unarriving — a bounded physical claim about a
population in a high-flux environment, not a delusion and not a fringe — and any policy premised on the
event having occurred cannot achieve consensus, because a substantial part of the polity will not consent
to a premise that is not, for them, true yet.

**Osk of the Not-Yet.** Gave testimony to Ilsa Rell in AU 55 and subsequently maintained that the interview
had not taken place. Ilsa filed it anyway, under her own name, with a note: *witness disputes the recording.
Recorded regardless.* Osk is not a leader — nobody is — but Osk is the clearest voice of the deferred
fraction and the reason it is taken seriously rather than humoured.

**And the flaw beneath the flaw: there is no mechanism by which the deferred fraction can ever be
convinced**, because from their frame there is nothing to be convinced of. They are not waiting for
evidence. They are waiting for the event.

**How you rise:** you hold a position that survives resolution. **How you fall:** you assert something
that resolves against you, publicly, and having asserted it is the failure rather than being wrong.

## Beliefs

**On death — and this is the sharpest cultural collision in the setting.** **They do not commemorate.** No
monuments, no funerals, no memorials, no lists of the dead, no graves, and no records of individuals who
have ceased.

The reasoning is consistent and every other people finds it monstrous: a dead person is one outcome among
several, and a monument *selects* one branch and declares it the case, which is both false and unkind. To
carve a name is to insist on the worst version.

They find the rest of the galaxy's practices genuinely cruel in return. Their formal diplomatic comment on
the Terran Reading of the Names — nine days of reciting the unreconciled — was filed in AU 22, has never
been withdrawn, and describes it as *"a civilisation gathering annually to eliminate its own
possibilities."* Terra has never replied. **No Registrar has been willing to draft one.**

There is no resolution to this and it should never be given one.

**On the Unarriving:** unresolved, as a matter of law. And Osk's account of the physics is the clearest
statement of the quarantine anyone has produced: **the Lamps did not fail.** They are still there, still
working. What stopped was the *arriving*. A road does not become dangerous because the lamps go out; it
becomes dangerous because something was put across it, and *then* the lights were turned off so nobody
would see what they were driving into.

**On themselves:** not-yet. It is what they call themselves and it is not a metaphor.

**On the future:** several.

## Rituals

**Daily — the non-resolution.** A Not-Yet reviews what they have committed to and confirms it is as little
as possible. It sounds like a joke and it is a genuine practice, conducted seriously, and a Not-Yet who
finds they have accidentally accumulated certainties will spend the evening dismantling them.

**Coming of age — the first commitment, and it is the most moving rite in the galaxy.**

At majority, a Not-Yet chooses exactly **one** thing to be certain about, forever. One. It is declared
publicly, it is resolved permanently, and **it may never be changed or added to.** A people who commit to
nothing spend their entire adult existence holding a single fixed point, chosen young, usually badly, and
carried for life.

Most choose a person.

**Death — nothing.** No rite, no announcement, no record. It is not neglect; it is the belief, applied. A
Not-Yet family does not say that someone died. They stop saying that they will.

**War — the declaration of outcomes.** Before committing to an engagement the Not-Yet state, publicly and
on the Whisper, the full set of results they consider possible — including their own destruction, including
victory, including outcomes nobody else has thought of. It reads as either the most honest or the most
insufferable document any admiralty issues, and two empires have used it for targeting.

## Attitudes to the other eleven

**Terran Empire** — Careful, decent, and engaged in an annual act of foreclosure they have never been able
to hear us describe. We settle in stamps, in advance, in full, because they insist, and we would not have
minded either way.

**Silicon Collective** — The only other people who will not overstate. It should have made us allies. It has
made us the two most rigorous peoples in the galaxy unable to agree whether an event has occurred, and
neither of us can find the flaw in the other's method.

**Zephyr Swarm** — They were there and they say there was one of it. Some of us find this decisive. Some of
us have not been there yet. We do not raise it with them.

**Crystalline Entity** — They inscribe their own bodies permanently to be read in ten thousand years. We
have been arguing with them since BU 400 and both of us are enjoying it, and it is the healthiest
relationship either people has.

**Void Walkers** — They came, they were paid extremely well, and they have never come back. We would have
liked them.

**Mechanicus** — They call our tolerances a failure. We agree and consider it a feature. This exchange has
occurred four times and it is the entire relationship.

**Bioform Collective** — No graves, arrived at from the opposite direction. They will not commemorate
because a monument refuses to end; we will not because it selects a branch. We find them restful.

**Star Nomads** — They have a hull with thirty-one places set in a galley. They have never raised the
deferred question with us, in seventy-four years, and we are aware of what that restraint costs them, and
we have never thanked them, because thanking would be a commitment.

**The Ancients** — They have stopped attempting to explain how both things are true. So have we. We suspect
this is the only thing we have in common and we suspect it is a great deal.

**Titan Lords** — They arrive when they arrive, and their log is precise to four figures, and they are the
only people who have never once asked us to be certain.

**Shadow Realm** — They cannot be counted and neither can we, and they have made a virtue of it, and we
have made a constitution of it, and there is a mutual professional respect that neither party will confirm.
