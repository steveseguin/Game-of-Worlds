# The Ancients

Pure lore. Place and economy are in `../12-civilisations/09-ancients.md`.

---

## Aesthetic

**Form language: maintained newness.** No ornament, and no wear. Surfaces maintained continuously for
millennia look *new*, which is far eerier than ruin — there is no patina anywhere, no worn thresholds, no
scuffed corners, nothing to indicate that anybody has ever walked here. It is not clean the way a hospital
is clean. It is clean the way something is clean that has never been used carelessly, not once, in ten
thousand years.

**And the proportions are wrong for anyone currently alive.** Doorways slightly too tall. Corridors
slightly too wide. Treads slightly too deep. Everything is *nearly* right, at a scale that suggests
something a little larger and a little slower. They were built for somebody, and it is not what maintains
them now, and no Ancient has ever been asked about this in a way that produced an answer.

**Palette:** warm neutral. No colour at all — not muted colour, *no* colour. Every other people in the
galaxy has a livery. The Ancients have a temperature.

**Insignia:** none. Not concealed; absent. They have never needed to identify themselves to anyone,
because there has never been another one of them to be confused with.

**People:** **nobody has seen an Ancient body.** The Emissary attends councils and is present and is
never described the same way twice in any surviving record, and this is not mystery-mongering — the
Registry's file contains nine mutually incompatible descriptions taken by competent officers, and no
explanation, and no follow-up, because asking felt discourteous.

Do not resolve this.

## Personality

Tired, courteous, and sorry.

This is the whole register and it should never vary. The Ancients are not sinister, not aloof, not
cryptic-for-effect. They are the most destructive force in galactic history and they are exhausted, and
they are polite to everybody, and they are extremely late.

**They use the past tense for present things.** Consistently, without exception. *There was an account. It
was very fine. We were wrong.* This is not affectation and it is not grief; it is what a people sound like
who have concluded that they are a completed thing. **Keep it absolutely consistent** — one slip into the
present tense would be a story event.

**Their humour:** there is none. Not because they are grave, but because a joke is an assertion about how
things are, and they have stopped making those.

**Their virtue:** they have never lied. Not once, in any surviving record. They warned everyone, in four
sentences, in BU 8, and the warning was true, and it was filed under shipping, and they have not
complained about that in eighty-two years.

**Their vice, and it is the one the whole setting turns on:** **they answered the question instead of the
person.** Ilsa Rell asked them thirty-one times and they declined every time on the grounds that they had
nothing to add to a thing they had already said — which was factually correct. She was not asking for
information. She was asking to be *answered*, by the only people who could. They worked that out. They
came. She had been dead six years.

*We have considered it since, and we were wrong about what she was asking for.*

## Leadership and structure — AU 74

**An order with duties, not a state with interests.** There is no head, no assembly, no succession, and no
visible internal disagreement. Function determines authority: the unit that holds the watch speaks for the
watch, and that is the entire constitution.

**The Emissary.** Has attended every galactic council since AU 3 and has spoken **twelve** times. The
twelfth was in AU 74, to Chart-Warden Rell, and ran to six sentences. That is the whole of Ancient foreign
policy: attendance, near-total silence, and a shrinking number of chairs.

**And the structure is failing in the least dramatic way available.** Their entire order is oriented
around a task they themselves terminated — they existed to maintain the Trellis, and in BU 61 they began
shutting it down. Seventy-four years later they are an institution whose purpose was completed by its own
hand.

There is no civil war. No reform faction, no revanchist wing, no succession crisis. There is **drift.**
Units stop. A facility goes quiet, and nobody is dispatched, and eleven years later the Registry notices
it has dropped off the Whisper. **Fourteen catalogued installations have gone silent since AU 40. None
has been reoccupied. None has been explained.**

**How you rise:** you hold a duty. **How you fall:** you stop, and nobody comes.

## Beliefs

**On death:** unknown, and never discussed, and the gap should stay. What is observable is that they do
not replace what is lost, and do not mark it, and do not appear to expect to.

**On the Unarriving:** they did it. They began the shutdown in BU 61 because the lanes ran both ways and
something was using them, and the cost was the Unarriving and the Shortening and seventy-four years of
dark, and they have carried it without explaining.

**Two reasons for the silence, both stated obliquely and neither ever confirmed.** An explanation is a
message, and a message can be read by the thing you are sealing out. And — worse, and the reason they have
stopped trying — **the shell is still expanding and they cannot stop it.** Whatever they set in motion
propagates on its own, will finish in nine hundred years, and there is nothing to negotiate, no help anyone
can offer, and no version in which telling anybody improves the outcome.

The Emissary's silence is not a secret being kept. It is somebody with nothing useful to say, attending
every meeting anyway, for seventy-four years.

**On themselves:** that they were the maintainers and not the builders. **This distinction is the
campaign's central revelation** and they will not confirm it until forced.

**On the future:** there is not one, for them. There may be one for everybody else, and that is what the
watch is for.

## Rituals

**Daily — nothing.** No observance, no marking of time, no acknowledgement of a day passing. Their
installations run and are maintained and that is all that happens.

**Coming of age — never observed.** No Ancient has been seen to begin. There is no record of one being
new.

**Death — never observed either.** Fourteen installations have gone quiet and in no case did anything
arrive to attend to it.

**The watch — and it is the only rite they have.** Somewhere past the frontier there is a relay, sited
adjacent to a collapsar such that it can only be observed from a position nothing survives. Since AU 0,
an Ancient unit has been watching it. Continuously. Not for enemies and not for salvage.

**They are watching to see whether the door is checked again.**

It was checked once, on the seventh day of the nine — one Lamp, back on for rather more than six hours, at
the far end, in good order — and Shipmaster Vaun of the Titan Lords is the only outside witness, and his
report was filed as instrument error. The Ancients know it happened. They have been watching for
seventy-four years for the second time.

**War — they do not fight wars.** They have never declared one, joined one, or been formally at one. When
attacked they defend, superbly and briefly, with hulls they cannot replace, and they do not pursue, and
they do not retaliate, and they do not mention it afterward.

## Attitudes to the other eleven

Written in their register. Every line is past tense, and every line is an answer to a question nobody
quite asked.

**Terran Empire** — They kept the letter. That was more than we expected of anyone. It was filed
incorrectly, which was correct.

**Silicon Collective** — They found the sequence. We knew they would; it was the only outcome the data
permitted. Nobody read it. We had also expected that.

**Zephyr Swarm** — They were in transit in enormous numbers. We were not able to prevent that. It was the
worst of the nine days and it is not in anyone's figure.

**Crystalline Entity** — They felt it. Of course they did. We would have told them if telling had been
available to us, and it was not, and Sarn has never asked, and we have noticed that Sarn has never asked.

**Void Walkers** — One of them turned. We did not think anybody would be fast enough to turn. She saw it,
and she never said, and that was the correct decision and we do not think she made it for the correct
reason.

**Mechanicus** — They are walking toward it. They have been walking toward it for two hundred years on a
schedule filed by units that no longer exist. We have no means of asking them to stop that they would
recognise as an instruction.

**Bioform Collective** — They knew forty years early and told nobody. They were right, and they were right
without knowing why, and it is the only thing in seventy-four years that has surprised us.

**Star Nomads** — We owe them a number. We do not have it. The count was kept by the Trellis and we did
not think to take a copy, and that is the single most careless thing we have ever done.

**Quantum Entities** — Some of them are correct that it has not happened. We are not able to explain how
both things are true and we have stopped attempting it.

**Titan Lords** — The slow one saw the Lamp relight. We did not know anybody was still inside. We have
read his log. It is accurate to four figures and it was filed as instrument error, and we did not
intervene, because intervening would have required us to say why it mattered.

**Shadow Realm** — They watched us at the relay in BU 13 and have been selling it in pieces for seventy
years. They cannot read it. If they assemble it they will, and we have not attempted to stop them, and we
would like the record to show that we have not.
