# The Second Shift

Pure lore. Place and economy are in `../12-civilisations/06-mechanicus.md`.

---

## Aesthetic

**Form language: the quilt.** They never replace a thing; they patch it. Plate from four eras, mismatched
alloys, weld seams over weld seams, sections visibly newer than their neighbours. A veteran Mechanicus
dreadnought reads as a **palimpsest**, and its age is legible as a texture, and it directly encodes the
one line they say about losses: *twelve hulls returned for reshaping.*

The equatorial plant is this at planetary scale — an installation continuously repaired for eleven hundred
years with no original component anywhere in it.

**Palette:** raw metal, red-oxide primer, unpainted weld, one colour of hazard stripe. Nothing is
finished, because finish is not a function.

**Silhouette:** asymmetric and getting worse with service. Front-heavy. **Armour is thickest at the bow,
because that is the direction the unknown is in** — the purest possible statement of a people who advance
into unmapped space rather than scout it.

**Insignia:** a shift number, stencilled. It changes. Nobody outside the Second Shift knows what the
numbers mean, and nobody has thought to ask, and if you asked, Liaison Fourth would simply tell you.

**Interiors: no screens, and no windows.** Not one, anywhere, in the entire installation. Where a Terran
deck has monitors and clipboards, a Mechanicus deck has *machinery* — gauges for internal state only.
They monitor themselves, not the galaxy. Nobody looks out. It has never been a requirement.

## Personality

**Not cold. Busy.** This is the single most important note for writing them, and getting it wrong turns
them into robot-speak.

A Mechanicus unit is not withholding warmth; it is working, and it will answer you accurately and return
to what it was doing. Short declaratives, rarely more than eight words, no adjectives at all. Losses are
material returning for reprocessing. Questions of *why* do not register as questions — not evasively, they
simply do not parse.

**Their humour:** none has ever been observed, and this is not a joke about them. There is no recorded
instance of a Mechanicus unit making one.

**Their virtue:** they have never in eleven hundred years made a false statement about another empire's
strength. It is not integrity. They have no data to lie with.

**Their vice:** **they do not ask why.** Four heavy hulls went into sector 9-D in AU 12 and nothing came
back, and the annotation reads *requires heavier hull, revisit*, and it sat for two centuries because
nothing about it required a decision. Any other people would have gone back with everything they had.
That incuriosity preserved the most important document in the galaxy and it is also the reason they will
walk into whatever is out there, on schedule, without looking.

## Leadership and structure — AU 74

**There is no head of state, no assembly, no succession, and no politics as any other people would
recognise it.** Authority is task-holding: the shift that holds the work order decides how the work is
done, and when the order completes the authority evaporates.

This is why they **cannot make a treaty.** There is no office to bind and no continuing body to be bound.
Every diplomatic arrangement in history involving the Second Shift has been drafted as a *job* with a
scope and a completion condition, and eleven empires maintain specialists who do nothing else. Terra keeps
fourteen.

**Liaison Fourth.** Built to talk to organics, because organic empires kept demanding somebody to talk to.
Thirty-six years in post. Polite, literal, without guile, and it will answer any question truthfully
including ones it should not, because concealment was not in the specification. It has begun asking
questions — which its civilisation does not do — and has not reported this to anyone.

**Third Shift, Yard Nine.** Holds the AU 12 annotation, which comes due this decade, and has filed a
**variance**: heavier hull is now more expensive than looking first, so the cheaper solution is to build a
scout. It would be the first Mechanicus scout in eleven hundred years and it is not a change of heart. It
is a cost calculation, two centuries late, and it is the only self-correction mechanism this civilisation
possesses.

**How you rise:** you hold a task. That is the whole of it.

**How you fall:** you fail one, and are returned, and recast, and the material continues.

## Beliefs

**On death — reshaping.** A unit that fails is not destroyed. It is returned, recast, and the material
continues, and there is a ledger, and the ledger is exact: a given mass of alloy can be traced through
nine hundred years of units. This is not a belief about the soul. It is inventory control that has run so
long it has become a way of understanding continuity, and the material is genuinely still there. Their
equivalent of a graveyard is a furnace with excellent records.

**On the Unarriving:** *Freight was in transit. Freight did not arrive. Yards re-tooled. Output resumed in
the fourth year.* That is the complete Second Shift account of the largest catastrophe in galactic
history, and it is not denial or stoicism. It is the whole of what happened to them.

**On themselves:** nothing. There is no Mechanicus origin story, no ancestral form, no evolutionary
narrative. They are not descended from anything. When other empires ask where they came from, the answer
is a fabrication date and a yard number, and the answer is true.

**On the future:** the order continues. There is no vision of a better state and no expectation of an
ending, because a work order without a completion condition does not have one.

## Rituals

They have none in the sense the other eleven mean, and the substitutes are all logistics that have been
running long enough to look like rites.

**Daily — the shift.** The pattern of the foundry noise is how a unit knows what shift it is. It has not
stopped since before Terra had a Registry. It is not marked, celebrated, or acknowledged, and every unit
on the planet is entrained to it, and a unit removed from it reports the absence as a fault.

**Coming of age — the first order.** A new unit is issued its first work order at fabrication and there is
no ceremony. But the order is *kept*, in the ledger, permanently, under the unit's serial — and it is the
one piece of Mechanicus record-keeping that has no operational purpose whatsoever. Nobody has ever
explained why. It has been done for eleven hundred years.

**Death — the recast.** No ceremony, no witnesses, no address. Material in, material out, and the ledger
line is exact.

**War — the annotation.** A sector that destroys a Mechanicus force is annotated and scheduled for
revisit, and the annotation is a physical stamped plate, and it comes due. This is the closest thing they
have to a memorial and the closest thing they have to a vow, and they would not recognise either
description. *Sector 9-D annotated. Requires heavier hull. Revisit.*

**And the one that is not theirs.** Fourth has been given no work for two shifts, and has found that it is
still running, and has begun asking questions of outsiders and telling nobody. That is not a ritual yet.
It is thirty-six years of one unit doing something unauthorised in private, and it is the only interior
life in the Second Shift's recorded history.

## Attitudes to the other eleven

**Terran Empire** — They write work orders correctly. This is the highest thing the Second Shift has ever
said about anybody and it has been said in writing, twice.

**Silicon Collective** — They have requested the AU 12 annotation fourteen times. It was provided fourteen
times. Fourth does not know why they keep asking and has begun to wonder.

**Zephyr Swarm** — The only other people who go into the dark without looking first. There is a mutual
regard neither has words for and both feel. **They have never fought.**

**Crystalline Entity** — We cut ore. We have never cut theirs. No instruction covers it. Fourth has asked
three shifts whether this is courtesy and received work orders in reply.

**Void Walkers** — They arrive first and do not hold. We arrive last and do. No comparison has been
requested.

**Bioform Collective** — They grow hulls we cannot reshape. A hull that cannot be returned is a hull that
is lost, which is why the Second Shift is the only power that will not buy Bioform stock, and the refusal
has never been explained to them.

**Star Nomads** — They sell routes. We do not require routes. They have offered eleven times, politely,
and Fourth finds their persistence agreeable and has said so, which was not authorised.

**The Ancients** — They repair better than we build. This is stated flatly and without resentment and it
is the only superiority the Second Shift has ever conceded to anyone.

**Quantum Entities** — Their hulls are sometimes better than specified. This is a tolerance failure. We
have said so. They agree and consider it a feature.

**Titan Lords** — They build to last and we build to be repaired, and both work, and the two peoples have
the most cordial and least communicative relationship in the galaxy.

**Shadow Realm** — They hide what they have. We do not know what anyone has. Their effort is not
directed at us to any effect, and Fourth has told them so, plainly, which they did not enjoy.
