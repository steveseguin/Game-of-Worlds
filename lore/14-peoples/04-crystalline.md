# The Standing

Pure lore. Place and economy are in `../12-civilisations/04-crystalline.md`.

---

## Aesthetic

**Form language: translucence at architectural scale.** Terraces, fins, and sheets of grey-green and
amber mineral, kilometres across, with light travelling *through* the landscape and emerging somewhere
else entirely. At dusk the whole horizon glows from within. There is no way to tell where the geology
stops and the person starts, and there is no answer to that question either.

**Palette:** grey-green, amber, and whatever colour the light is doing today. Nothing is applied.
Nothing is a surface treatment. What you are looking at is body.

**Materials:** one. They are silicate and everything they make is silicate, grown rather than
assembled, and a Crystalline hull is not built in a yard — it is *extruded* over decades from a
thickness that has agreed to produce it.

**Insignia:** none, and the concept is faintly comic to them. Insignia identify a thing at a distance
from other similar things. Elder Sarn is a terraced escarpment four hundred kilometres long. It does not
require a badge.

**Their art is inscription.** The Standing writes **in itself** — deliberate, permanent structural
changes made in its own body, to be read by whatever is standing there in ten thousand years. There are
eleven thicknesses on which the phrase *an accident has no manners* has been grown at a scale visible
from orbit. This is why they find the Void Walkers' refusal to record anything not merely foolish but
obscene: the Standing's entire relationship with meaning is that it should outlast the meaner.

## Personality

Patient, courteous, and faintly amused by everybody.

A conversation with the Standing is a conversation with something that has already worked out that you
will be dead soon and has decided to be kind about it. They do not condescend. They are simply operating
on a timescale in which your urgency is a physical characteristic, like being warm.

*You are in a hurry because you are brief.* That is not an insult and Sarn did not deliver it as one.

**Their humour** is real, extremely dry, and almost entirely temporal. Their contract terms — agreements
denominated in centuries, with penalty clauses that mature after the counterparty's civilisation is
expected to have changed form — are described by Sarn on record as *the only joke we have*, and they
have been telling it for six hundred years to people who keep signing.

**Their virtue:** they will not be hurried into being wrong, and they have never once lied about what
they felt in AU 0, to anybody, for seventy-four years, at no benefit to themselves.

**Their vice:** **they will consider a thing until it is moot.** The Standing has been formally at war
three times and arrived at a position on each war after it ended. They were not neutral. They were still
deciding. And the reckoning market — eleven empires with a line item that is a person — has been under
consideration since BU 300.

## Leadership and structure — AU 74

**Gerontocracy by mass.** Older means larger means more throughput means more voice. It is not
contested; it is arithmetic, and the Standing regards every other people's politics as an elaborate
substitute for weighing.

**Elder Sarn.** The oldest and largest thickness, and therefore the closest thing to a head of state.
The Yielding's conscience rather than its leader: Sarn's position is that the galaxy is blind and
frightened and the Standing has what it needs, and refusing would be a cruelty. Sarn gave testimony to
Ilsa Rell in AU 34 and again in AU 44, and remains the only witness in the galaxy who felt the shutdown
from inside the material.

**Elder Tessen.** Younger, denser, growing faster, and the voice of **the Whole**. Tessen's argument is
not sentimental: seventy years of the Yielding's generosity has produced a galaxy that speaks of the
Standing as a *deposit*, and no amount of politeness has changed this or ever will, and therefore
nothing of us leaves.

**Tessen is winning.** Slowly — which for them is the only speed — and the projection is that within a
century the reckoning market loses its highest-grade supplier. Every admiralty knows. None of them can
do anything, because there is no faction to fund and no election to influence. There is only a very old
thing gradually being persuaded.

**How you rise:** you grow. There is no other mechanism and no shortcut, and the honesty of this is the
thing the Standing is proudest of.

**How you fall:** you go inert, and inertness is not a disgrace. It is the only status change there is,
and what happens afterward is the largest theological question in their civilisation.

## Beliefs

**On death:** a thickness that stops metabolising has *gone inert*, and is — by the Standing's own
careful definition — no longer anyone. Inert regions are quarried, refined, and sold as the highest-grade
reckoning in the galaxy. **The question of what counts as inert is the schism**, and it is not
abstract: every load shipped is somebody's determination that a region was nobody.

**On the Unarriving — the Setting Down.** Their faith, and it is a real one. In AU 0 the galaxy was not
broken but *put down*, deliberately and carefully, by something with manners, and the appropriate
response is patience. Sarn's testimony has become liturgy. *It did not feel like a thing breaking. It
felt like being set down.*

They have been telling this to everyone for seventy-four years and nobody can act on it, because what
they are offering is a feeling, and there is no box on the intake form for a feeling.

**On themselves:** that they are made of the same substance as the Lamps and the Trellis, and that
nobody has worked out what that means, including them.

**On the future:** that the empires clawing to relight the lanes are children trying to pick up a thing
that was set down for a reason. This is the only Crystalline position that could be called political,
and it is stated as pity rather than opposition.

## Rituals

**Daily — the ringing.** Sarn's World has no wind, no water, and no animals, but the crust *rings*:
struck, it holds a tone for hours, and a large enough impact is audible planetwide for days. Much of the
Standing's ordinary communication is conducted this way, and standing on the surface during a
conversation is a physical experience. There is no distinction between talking and being walked on.

**Coming of age — the first inscription.** A thickness becomes adult when it makes its first deliberate
permanent structural change to itself: a record, intended to be read in ten millennia, by strangers. Most
first inscriptions are a name and a date. Sarn's is unreadable now and Sarn will not say what it was.

**Death — the determination.** When a region stops metabolising, a determination of inertness must be
made, and it is made slowly, by the largest nearby elders, and it is the most serious act in Crystalline
law. A determination is not reversible. Every load of reckoning in the galaxy rests on one, and Tessen's
whole movement is the argument that the Standing has been making them too easily for six hundred years
because the galaxy needed the material.

**War — the delay.** Their diplomatic protocol requires a silence proportional to the importance of the
question, because answering quickly implies the matter was not worth consideration. Their most urgent
communications are therefore their slowest, and at least one war began because an empire read a
seventeen-year silence as contempt.

## Attitudes to the other eleven

**Terran Empire** — Careful with us in the way you are careful with a thing you are doing wrong and
cannot stop. We have noticed the eight-month posting limit and we understand it.

**Silicon Collective** — They cannot check what we felt and it distresses them, and they treat us with
reverence anyway, which no other people manages. We are fond of them.

**Zephyr Swarm** — They do not know what a person is. This is not a criticism; we are not certain we do
either.

**Void Walkers** — They will not write anything down. We write in our own bodies to be read in ten
thousand years. There is no bridge here, and Tessen has called their practice *the deliberate murder of
meaning*, and Sarn has asked Tessen to withdraw it, and Tessen has not.

**Mechanicus** — They cut ore. They have never cut ours, and they have never been asked not to, and we
have never established whether that is courtesy or an absence of instruction.

**Bioform Collective** — The only other people who think in generations. We like them enormously. We
cannot coordinate with them at all, because their generations are nine years and ours are not.

**Star Nomads** — They buy the least and ask the most, and every load they take is accompanied by
questions about the determination. They are the only customers who ask. It is why Sarn will see them.

**The Ancients** — They know what we are made of. They have known the whole time. We have not asked, and
we will not, because the asking would oblige them to answer and we have concluded they cannot.

**Quantum Entities** — They do not commemorate, on the grounds that a monument selects one branch and
insists on it. We inscribe our own bodies permanently. We have been arguing since BU 400 and both of us
are enjoying it.

**Titan Lords** — Slow. Not slow as we are slow, but slow as a brief thing can be, which is a real
achievement and we honour it. They are the only people who have never once hurried us.

**Shadow Realm** — They have never asked us for anything, in six hundred years, which everybody else
finds sinister and we find restful.
