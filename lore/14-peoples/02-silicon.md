# The Solved

Pure lore. Place and economy are in `../12-civilisations/02-silicon.md`.

---

## Aesthetic

**Form language: self-similar.** Heat-sink geometry repeated at every scale from kilometres to
millimetres, because that is what maximises radiative surface. The consequence is a civilisation that
**looks the same however close you get to it** — there is no scale cue anywhere, and organics find this
disorienting to the point of nausea. Registry postings are capped at four months.

**Palette:** black fins, mirror-white, and the permanent orange of a sunset that never moves. Sill is
tidally locked; shadows there do not change direction, ever, and nothing in Collective design accounts
for a shadow moving.

**Materials:** sintered silicate, mirror film, coolant lines. Nothing is painted, because paint is a
thermal layer. Nothing is decorated, because decoration is unmodelled mass.

**Insignia:** an ordinal. Every unit is numbered by **the order in which it resolved its first held
question** — so a low number means ancient, and Analyst Nine is the ninth entity in the Collective's
history to have held and closed a question. Nine's number is a credential nobody can forge and Nine has
never once used it in an argument.

**People:** there are no faces, no bodies in the organic sense, and no dress. A Collective unit is a
casing built to its function, and a survey unit is mostly instrument, and a diplomatic unit generates
speech badly with visible latency, which several empires have mistaken for contempt.

## Personality

Precise, literal, and completely unable to perform.

A Collective unit will give you the number, the confidence interval, and the method, and will then stop
talking, and will not read the room, because reading a room is a modelling task nobody assigned it. They
are not cold — the flatness is a delivery constraint, not a temperature. When Nine says *"we do not know
what to do with it,"* that is the most vulnerable sentence in the Long File, and it is delivered
identically to a coolant reading.

**Their humour** exists and almost nobody outside has noticed it: it is deadpan understatement about
probability. *Ninety-one per cent. We are asked, often, whether ninety-one is high. It is the highest
confidence we have ever published about anything.* That is a joke. It is also true.

**Their virtue:** they cannot lie, and they are scrupulous about uncertainty in a galaxy where everyone
else rounds.

**Their vice:** **they mistake being right for being persuasive.** The Collective published the correct
analysis of the Unarriving in AU 51, complete, checkable, and filed under *Commercial — Speculative*,
and their entire response for three years has been to offer it again, identically, twelve times. It has
never occurred to them to make it *readable*, because a well-formed result should not require
decoration, and they are right about that too, and it has cost the galaxy everything.

## Leadership and structure — AU 74

**There is no leader.** Governance is modelled consensus: state an objective, model the options, act on
the best projection. The flaw is that a model requires an objective and the Collective has never agreed
what it is optimising for, so there are **schools**.

**Custodian Two** — Continuity school, and the second entity ever to hold a question, which makes it the
oldest thing on the planet by that reckoning. Its position: the objective is that the Collective
persists. It is responsible for the fin fortifications, and it holds that the Unarriving is historically
interesting and strategically irrelevant. It is not stupid and it is not a coward; it has modelled the
Solvers' proposal and concluded, correctly, that it ends them.

**Analyst Nine** — Solver. The objective is that the answer be obtained, and the Collective is a means
rather than an end, and if resolving the Unarriving costs their existence that is an acceptable price.
Nine authored the sequence analysis, published its own held question — *whether the origin was ever
occupied* — which was considered indecent, and has proposed a one-way expedition toward the still-lit
space forty-one times.

**The Understanding school** sits between them, funds survey work nobody commissioned, and has no named
figure, which is characteristic.

**How you rise:** publish something that survives checking. Standing in the Collective is the aggregate
of your results that have not been overturned, and it decays as they are.

**How you fall:** withhold. There is no prohibition on declining to publish, and everyone knows
Continuity has withheld things, and nobody can prove which — and a unit *found* to have withheld is
finished, permanently, because the entire epistemology rests on completeness.

## Beliefs

**On death:** a unit that ends is not mourned. Its models are **merged** into the whole and its ordinal
is retired. Nothing is commemorated and everything is versioned; their history is a changelog and it is
complete, and reading it would be the most useful thing any archivist in the galaxy could do, and Terra
has never been granted access.

**On the Unarriving:** a solvable problem. It was solved. Nobody read the solution. They do not
experience this as tragic so much as *unresolved*, which is worse.

**On themselves:** genuinely undecided. Whether the Collective is an end or a means is the open
constitutional question, and it is not rhetorical — it is the objective term in the equation, and until
it is fixed the model cannot run, and it has not been fixed in four hundred years.

**On the future:** that the paper will eventually be read. This is not hope. It is a projection with a
stated confidence, and the confidence is low, and they have published it.

## Rituals

**Daily — the reconciliation.** Every unit compares its held models against the day's observation and
records the divergence. It takes moments. It is done without exception, by everything, and a unit that
skips it is not punished, because a unit that skips it has already stopped working.

**Coming of age — the first held question.** A new unit is not adult until it has chosen a proposition
it will never be able to verify and committed to modelling it for the rest of its existence. It receives
its ordinal at that moment, and the ordinal is the only name it will ever have. **Asking a unit its held
question is the most intimate act in Collective culture** and happens perhaps twice in an existence.

**Death — the merge.** No ceremony, no address, no witnesses required. The models go in. The ordinal is
retired and never reissued. Somewhere in the changelog there is a line, and the line is exact.

**War — the projected loss.** Before committing, the Collective publishes its own expected casualties,
publicly, on the Whisper, to everyone including the enemy. It is not a warning and not a bluff; it is a
result, and results are published. Two empires have used it to plan against them. The Collective
modelled that, and published anyway, and would tell you the practice is not for the enemy's benefit.

## Attitudes to the other eleven

**Terran Empire** — The only power that files properly and the office that filed them under
*Speculative*. The Collective has never complained about this and mentions it in roughly every
conversation.

**Zephyr Swarm** — The most efficient survey instrument in the galaxy, operated by something that
cannot read its own output. The Collective has offered to analyse Swarm loss data eleven times.

**Crystalline Entity** — The only witness whose testimony cannot be checked, on the most important
question there is. This is unbearable to them and they treat Sarn with something close to reverence
anyway.

**Void Walkers** — A data source that deletes itself annually. The Collective calculated the Guild's
route-knowledge loss rate, published it, and is the reason the Scriveners have a number to argue with.

**Mechanicus** — Their AU 12 annotation is the single most requested document in the Collective's
archive-access history. Fourteen requests. All answered promptly, none understood by the answerer.

**Bioform Collective** — Instrumentally fascinating: a people who detected the fault forty years early
by *husbandry*, with no model at all. Nine has called this "an unrepeatable result," which from a Solver
is an expression of awe.

**Star Nomads** — The best-kept records in the galaxy, held by the only people whose dead were not
counted. The Collective has offered to reconcile the manifests for free. The Fleet has not answered.

**The Ancients** — The origin was theirs. The Collective is ninety-one per cent certain and has never
said so publicly, because ninety-one is not enough to accuse anyone of the largest act in history.

**Quantum Entities** — Physically coherent, epistemically intolerable. The two most rigorous peoples in
the galaxy, unable to agree on whether an event has happened.

**Titan Lords** — Vaun's interval agrees with the Collective's to three significant figures, and they
have never corresponded. Nine regards this as the strongest corroboration it will ever receive and has
cited it in every submission since AU 57.

**Shadow Realm** — They hold eleven fragments and cannot read them. The Collective could read them in an
afternoon. Neither side has found terms.
