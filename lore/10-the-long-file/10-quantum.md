# Testimony 10 — Quantum Entities

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Osk of the Not-Yet |
| **Was** | A fraction for whom the Unarriving has not occurred |
| **Recorded** | AU 55. Osk maintains the recording has not happened |
| **Carries** | Describes the Unarriving in **future tense** — including one arrival that is not on the schedule |

---

You would like an account of the nine days.

We would give you one, if we had had them.

Please do not sigh. You have sighed. We have been sighed at by eleven empires and it does not improve
the physics. A meaningful fraction of us has not experienced your catastrophe, and we are that
fraction, and we are not being difficult — we are being accurate, which is frequently mistaken for
the same thing.

So we cannot tell you what happened. We can tell you what will.

We would rather you wrote this down carefully, because we have found that people write down the
strange part and omit the useful part.

The Lamps will not fail.

That is the useful part and nobody ever takes it. The Lamps will still be there, and they will still
be doing the thing they do, and if you were to go and stand beside one you would find it in perfect
order. What will stop is not the lighting.

**What will stop is the arriving.**

We understand this is a distinction without a difference to you. It is the only difference there is.
A road does not become dangerous because the lamps go out. A road becomes dangerous because something
has been put across it, and the lamps are then turned off so that nobody sees what they are driving
into, and we would ask you to consider which of those two things somebody would do on purpose.

There is one more item and we would prefer to give it and stop.

For nine days nothing will arrive anywhere.

And then something will arrive.

One arrival, and it will not be on the schedule, and it will not be one of ours or one of yours, and
we have not been able to establish where. We would rather not be present for it.

We may not have been.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Quantum.** Conditional and subjunctive throughout, tenses that do not agree with the
interview taking place, and a final line that quietly withdraws the whole testimony. They are
maddening and they are never *evasive* — every sentence is a good-faith attempt at accuracy in a
grammar the listener cannot use. Kept short, as the roster requires, or it becomes unreadable.

**Two facts, and the second one is the file's darkest.**

1. *The Lamps did not fail.* Something was put across the road and then the lights were turned off so
   nobody would see it. This reframes the entire event: the Ancients did not break the infrastructure,
   they **blinded** the galaxy — which is exactly what you would do if the alternative were twelve
   species driving into a thing at light speed, forever.
2. *One arrival that is not on the schedule.* Never located, never described, never resolved in this
   testimony. It pairs with Vaun's log (testimony 11): the one Lamp that came back on for some hours
   at the far end, like a door being checked. Something is already inside.

   **And it is not the ship in testimony 8.** This is worth being exact about, because the resemblance
   is a trap. The *Ninefold Patience* returned in **AU 31**; Osk was recorded in **AU 55**. A Quantum
   witness using the future tense about a twenty-four-year-old event would be ordinary grammar and
   would mean nothing. Osk is not doing that. There is a second one, it is still ahead, and this is the
   only line in Series 9 that points at the player's own decade.

   Placed at the end of `../28-through-lines.md` T1 for that reason: the line runs *refusal* (Ossa,
   BU 40) → *handling* (Sarn, AU 0) → *a door checked* (Vaun, AU 0) → *a thing returned* (Keth, AU 31)
   → *a thing still coming* (Osk, AU 55). Five species, five doctrines, one escalation, and no
   character has read it as one.

**The road analogy** is the clearest statement of the quarantine in the whole file, and it is
delivered by the least credible witness in the galaxy, to an interviewer who has been sighing. That
is the correct way to place your most important exposition — in the mouth of someone nobody believes.

**VO note.** Brisk, faintly exasperated, entirely reasonable. Not ethereal, not dreamy, no reverb. The
last line — *"We may not have been"* — is thrown away, not landed. If the performer gives it weight
the joke and the horror both collapse.

**Art beat.** A Lamp in perfect working order, dark. Nothing wrong with it at all. That single image
carries testimony 10 without a word of it.
