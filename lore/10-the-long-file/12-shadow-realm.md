# Testimony 12 — Shadow Realm

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Sable, of the Unlisted |
| **Was** | A broker. Nothing further is offered |
| **Recorded** | AU 67 — one year before Ilsa's death |
| **Carries** | Imagery of Ancient vessels at the relay in BU 13 — sold in fragments for seventy years |

---

What a pleasure. Thirty years, and you have finally come to us. Do sit.

You will want to know where we were during the nine days.

What a good question. Do you know what I find remarkable about your file, Warden? You have eleven
testimonies and not one of them accuses anybody. Eleven species, several million dead, and your
cabinet contains no defendant at all. That is either the most honest archive in the galaxy or the most
useless one, and I have not decided.

You are asking again.

No — forgive me. You asked something slightly different that time. I noticed. It was well done.

Let me offer you something instead. It is not what you asked for, and it is better than what you asked
for, and I would like you to notice that I am *giving* it to you rather than selling it, because that
has not happened before and it will not happen again.

There is a relay. It is not on your charts. It is sited adjacent to a mouth — deliberately adjacent,
in the sense that it can only be observed from a position nothing survives. I invite you to think
about that at your leisure.

In BU 13, there were vessels at it.

Ancient vessels. Eleven of them. Working. For some months.

We have imagery.

You are about to ask why nobody else has it. That is the wrong question, so I will answer the right
one: everybody else has *some* of it. We have been selling it for seventy years. A frame here. A frame
there. Eleven buyers, eleven fragments, and not one buyer holding enough to make a sentence out of.

That was not caution. That was pricing.

Here is your fragment. It is a good one — I have chosen kindly, which is more than I have done for
anybody. It shows one vessel. It is timestamped. It shows nothing being done.

You will want the rest.

You will not have the rest, and I will give you the honest reason instead of a clever one: **we cannot
read them either.** Eleven fragments, seventy years, and we do not know what they mean. The day
somebody assembles them is the day the only thing we have stops being ours.

We are not what they say we are, Warden. You know this. You are the only one of yours who has never
written it down.

Do come back.

You will not, I think.

You look tired.

Give my regards to your grandchild.

---

## Notes

**Why it's a Shadow.** Courteous throughout and it never once answers the question asked. Ilsa's
questions are never printed — you can reconstruct all three from the deflections, and the second one
(*"you asked something slightly different that time. I noticed."*) tells you she was good at this. This
is the testimony that demonstrates the interviewer's skill without her speaking a word.

**The facts.** The relay, sited so it can only be observed from inside a mouth — which is testimony 4
in `06-campaign.md`, the discovery in mission 4, arriving here as a seventy-year-old open secret. And
Ancient vessels working at it in BU 13, five years before the four-sentence letter: **they were building
the shutdown before they warned anybody.**

**The best thing about their position.** They are innocent of the Unarriving, universally blamed for
it, cannot prove otherwise, and hold the single most incriminating evidence about somebody else — which
they have been monetising in fragments rather than using, because leverage you spend is leverage you no
longer have. Guilty of profiteering, innocent of the crime. Far more useful than a villain.

**The last line is the coldest in the file.** Sable knows about Rell. Ilsa dies within the year, and her
grandchild inherits the cabinet — and eventually reads this transcript and finds themselves mentioned in
it, by name, by somebody who has never met them, seven years before they took the post.

**"You look tired."** Sable is the only witness who notices. It is not a threat and it is not kindness;
it is an observation from someone who observes for a living, and it is the last thing anyone says to
Ilsa Rell in the file she spent forty years on.

**VO note.** Warm, delighted, unhurried, never sinister. The entire performance is hospitality. If the
performer plays menace, the character disappears.

**Art beat.** The fragment. One frame of imagery, timestamped BU 13, one Ancient vessel at a relay, and
nothing happening in it. It is genuinely useless on its own — and that is the point, and the player
should be given it early and told nothing.
