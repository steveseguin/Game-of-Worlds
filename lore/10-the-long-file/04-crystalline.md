# Testimony 4 — Crystalline Entity

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Elder Sarn of the Standing |
| **Was** | Present. That is the whole of its qualification |
| **Recorded** | AU 34, and again AU 44 — Ilsa returned |
| **Carries** | It did not feel like breaking. It felt like being **set down** — carefully, by someone |

---

You are brief.

I do not say it to wound you. I say it because you will want me to describe a moment, and I do not
have moments. I have durations, and the shortest duration I am able to notice is longer than your
grandmother's life.

So. The nine days. To me the nine days were an instant. It is the only instant I have ever had, and
I have spent what you would call seventy-four years turning it over.

We felt it. All of the Standing, everywhere, at once. You must understand that we are the same
substance. Whatever the Lamps were made of, we are made of it. Whatever the Trellis was grown from,
we were grown from. No, we do not know what that means either. Yes, we have been asked. Yes, the
asking is always very polite.

You want to know whether it hurt.

It did not hurt. That is what I have come to tell you, and it has taken me forty years to be
certain enough to say it out loud.

It did not feel like a thing breaking. I have felt things break. A seam goes, and there is a shear,
and it is fast and it is stupid, and afterward there is a wrongness in the shape of you. That is
breaking. This was not that.

**It felt like being set down.**

Carefully. The way you would set down something you did not wish to damage and did not intend to
pick up again soon. There was — I am going to use a word you will not like. There was
*consideration* in it.

Somebody put the galaxy down gently, Warden. I do not know who. I do not know why. I am not going
to speculate, because speculation is a brief creature's habit and I have no talent for it.

But it was not an accident. An accident has no manners.

You are writing quickly. You want to ask whether I told anyone. I told everyone. I have told every
empire that has ever sent anyone to us, for seventy-four years, and each of them thanked me and
wrote it down and went away, because what I have given them is a feeling, and there is nothing to
be done with a feeling.

You are the first one to ask me twice.

I will still be here. Come back with better questions.

Take your time. You will not. But take it.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Crystalline.** Measures in durations, treats seventy-four years as an instant, refuses
to speculate on principle, and opens by pointing out that the interviewer is short-lived. Never
raises its voice, never explains its people. The politeness of the mining question is mentioned
once and dropped, which is far more pointed than a complaint.

**The fact.** *An accident has no manners.* This is the testimony that establishes the shutdown was
deliberate — not through evidence, but through the only witness in the galaxy made of the same
material as the thing that was switched off. Nobody can check it. Everybody has to decide whether
to believe a rock about its feelings.

**Two recording dates.** AU 34 and AU 44 — Ilsa went back ten years later, which is why Sarn says
*"you are the first one to ask me twice."* One line, and it characterises the collector rather than
the witness. Use this trick sparingly; it works because it is the only place in the file it happens.

**VO note.** Slow, but not ponderous — the trap is playing a mountain. Sarn is *patient*, and warm,
and slightly amused by the interviewer. The last three lines are a kindness, and should be played
as one.

**Art beat.** No portrait, or barely one. A surface, at a scale that does not resolve into a face.
The visual idea is that the player cannot tell whether they are looking at a person or a landscape,
which is also the galactic reckoning market's whole problem.
