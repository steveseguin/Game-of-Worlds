# Testimony 9 — The Ancients

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | the Emissary |
| **Was** | Attended every galactic council since AU 3. Has spoken twelve times |
| **Recorded** | **AU 74** — the campaign's present. Taken by Chart-Warden Rell, not by Ilsa |
| **Carries** | Six sentences. A confession, if you can read it |

---

We were asked for an account.

There was an account: it was given in the eighth year before, in four sentences, to every empire at
once, and it was filed under shipping.

We do not have another, and we have never had another.

Your grandmother asked thirty-one times, and we did not answer, and that was not unkindness — there
was nothing to add to a thing we had already said.

We have considered it since, and we were wrong about what she was asking for.

You are welcome to the one we gave.

---

## Notes

**The shortest entry in the file, and the last.** Six sentences. It is the twelfth testimony, it
closes Series 9, and it is the only one Rell took personally.

**Why it's the Ancients.** Past tense for present things, throughout, without exception — *"there was
an account,"* not *"we have an account."* Institutional first-person plural. No explanation offered
and none withheld with any visible effort, because from their side there is genuinely nothing more to
say: they told everyone, once, in BU 8, and the telling was the whole of what they could safely do.

**The confession is in sentence five.** *"We were wrong about what she was asking for."*

Read it twice. Ilsa was not asking for information — she had spent forty years discovering that
information does not help. She was asking to be *answered*, by the only people who could have, and
they declined thirty-one times on the grounds that they had nothing to add. They were right about the
facts and wrong about the request, and they have worked that out, and they have come, and they are
seventy-four years and one funeral too late.

That is the closest thing to an apology this civilisation is capable of producing, and it gives the
player not one usable fact.

**Why they came.** Never stated. The player will assume it is because of something they did in Act
III, and that reading is available and probably correct. The better reading, never confirmed, is that
they came because Ilsa died and stopped asking.

**VO note.** The one place in the project where a second performer is genuinely worth arguing about
— and the answer is still no. Rell reads it, and Rell reads it *verbatim and at length*, which is the
handling reserved for the Emissary alone (see `05-characters.md`). Rell does not know what to make of
it and will not paraphrase what they cannot parse. The player should notice the change in handling
before they understand why.

Direct Rell's delivery as careful rather than moved. They are reading a document addressed to their
grandmother. Nothing else is required.

**Art beat.** The four-sentence letter and this testimony side by side on the same desk, in the same
hand-log, eighty-two years apart. That is the whole plot in one still image and it needs no caption.
