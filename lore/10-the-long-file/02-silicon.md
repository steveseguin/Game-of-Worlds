# Testimony 2 — Silicon Collective

| | |
|---|---|
| **Witness** | Analyst Nine of the Solved |
| **Was** | Author of the paper nobody read |
| **Recorded** | AU 57 |
| **Carries** | The Lamps failed in a **sequence**, propagating outward in a shell from one origin — and the origin is empty space |

---

We were asked for an account. We do not keep accounts. We have a result, and we will give it, and
we would like it noted that we have now given it twelve times.

The Lamps did not go out at once.

Every empire's history says they did. Every empire's history is drawing on its own local
observation, which in each case is a single point, and a single point cannot show a sequence. This
is not a criticism. It is arithmetic.

We had four thousand points.

The failures propagate. They propagate at a rate we can state to four significant figures, and we
will not state it here, because it has been published, and the publication was filed by your
Registry under *Commercial — Speculative*. They propagate outward, in a shell, from a single
origin. We have the origin to within two-tenths of a sector.

The origin is not a star.

We wish to be precise, because we have been misquoted on this point by people who were being kind
to us. It is not a dim star. It is not an uncatalogued star. It is not a star we have failed to
find. There is nothing there. There has never been anything there. We hold four centuries of
Concord survey data across that volume, from when it was routine and lit and crossed eleven times
a day, and it is empty in all four hundred years of it.

Something in an empty place switched off the Lamps in an expanding shell, at a stated rate,
beginning in BU 61.

The shell has not stopped. It is still expanding. It will complete in approximately nine hundred
years.

There are Lamps outside it that are still lit.

We would like that sentence read twice, and we recognise that we have no way of making anyone do
so. There is lit space in this galaxy. It is nine hundred years away and it is receding, and no
empire has sent anything toward it, and we have proposed it forty-one times.

Ninety-one per cent. That is our confidence. We are asked, often, whether ninety-one is high. It
is the highest confidence we have ever published about anything.

We are told our lack of feeling is why the paper was not read. We have modelled that claim and it
is likely correct, and we do not know what to do with it.

We are not asking to be believed, Warden. We are asking to be checked.

---

## Notes

**Why it's Silicon.** Plural throughout, no singular pronoun, probabilities in ordinary speech, and
the entire testimony is a *result* rather than a story — they open by rejecting the premise of being
interviewed. They never say "we believe." They say "ninety-one per cent."

**The two facts.** The origin coordinate, which is the keystone of the whole file — and the lit
space still out there, nine hundred years away and receding. That second one is the most
heartbreaking sentence in the setting: **safety exists, and it cannot be reached.**

**Note for canon:** this establishes that the shutdown is *ongoing*, not a single event in AU 0.
The Ancients initiated something that propagates and they cannot stop it. That deepens the
quarantine rather than contradicting it, and it means the Ancients' silence is not stubbornness
but futility.

**VO note.** No affect, but *not* robotic — the difference is warmth of tone against flatness of
delivery. Nine is not cold; Nine is unable to perform. The line "we do not know what to do with
it" is the only vulnerable moment and must not be played as one.

**Campaign use.** The origin coordinate is adjacent to the sector in Fourth's annotation
(testimony 6). Nobody in the world has put those two documents side by side. That is the player's
discovery and it should never be handed to them.
