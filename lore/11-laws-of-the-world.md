# The Laws of the World

Status: **LOCKED** where marked. This is the constitution.

Everything in this folder must obey this document, and so must every future mission script,
tooltip, race blurb and line of dialogue. Its purpose is to make the setting *load-bearing*: a
world where anything is possible has no drama in it, because nothing costs anything.

There is no magic in this galaxy. There are three civilisations that look like magic — the
Crystalline Entity, the Quantum Entities, the Void Walkers — and each is given hard limits below
precisely so that they cannot be used to solve a problem the rules say is unsolvable.

**The test for any new idea:** if it lets someone avoid paying a price this document says they
must pay, it is wrong, no matter how good it sounds.

---

## I · Flight

**1. Superluminal transit is blind. LOCKED.**
A ship above light speed receives no information about what is ahead of it. Information about the
space in front arrives at the same instant the ship does. This is absolute. No race, no tech
level, no ability, no story beat gets an exception.

**2. Deceleration buys a few seconds of sight, and nothing else. LOCKED.**
A ship whose destination is a sector is slowing on approach and gets a brief look. This is the
entire reason arrival is safer than transit (25% versus 50% per hull). It is seconds, not minutes,
and it cannot be extended by any technology.

**3. Departure from held space is free.** You are accelerating out through space you have already
charted. Never rolled. There is no code for it and none should be written.

**4. Kinetic hazard cannot be shielded. LOCKED.**
Deflectors work by making *aimed* shots miss. A shoal at relativistic closing speed is not aimed
and not dodgeable, and a Crystalline hull with the best shields in the galaxy rolls the same odds
as a Zephyr frigate. This is why the hazard roll ignores shields in the engine, and it must stay
that way — it is the one place where technology cannot buy safety, and it is what keeps the map
frightening at every tech level.

**5. A collapsar is not survivable by anything, ever. LOCKED.** No roll. No hull. No ability.

**6. Sweeping is permanent and it is the only way to make space safe.**
Hold a shoal and it is charted, cleared, corridored, and safe forever. This is the single
positive-sum mechanic in the setting and the fiction should treat it as sacred.

---

## II · Knowing

**7. There is no faster-than-light sensing.** You cannot look at a distant sector. Light takes
years. Every fact about unheld space was obtained by sending something there.

**8. Espionage is human, not remote.** The Intel branch is agents, bribes, bought charts and
purchased crew. It is never clairvoyance. When you "see an enemy's sectors," you are reading a
copy of their chart, which means it can be out of date, and which means it can be *poisoned*.

**9. Counter-intelligence works by lying, not by shooting.** A destroyed probe was salted — given
a false trace, which it flew. This matters: the probe was not intercepted, it was *deceived*, and
somebody wrote the false chart by hand.

**10. A trace is property and it degrades.** A route survived once is a fact about one crossing.
Shoals drift. A twenty-year-old trace is a rumour with paperwork.

---

## III · Talking — the Whisper

**11. There is no FTL cargo, but there IS FTL voice. LOCKED.**

This is the most important rule in the document and it exists to make the galaxy's politics
possible.

The Trellis had two layers. The **Lamps** let ships see, and they are gone. The **relays** carried
signal — voice, text, numbers — and they still work. Nobody knows why one layer failed and the
other did not, and the Silicon Collective's answer is that the relays were never the point and
whatever shut the Trellis down had no reason to touch them.

The surviving relay net is called **the Whisper**.

- It carries **signal only**. Voice, text, ledgers, treaty language.
- It cannot carry **cargo, people, or sight**. You can talk to an empire you can never visit.
- It cannot **guide** a ship. It tells you nothing about the space between you.
- It is **not private**. Everyone assumes everyone listens, and everyone is right.

**What this buys us.** It is why twelve empires can negotiate, trade, insult each other and run a
shared archive while being physically unable to reach one another. It is why the Registry works.
It is why the player has a chat channel and a diplomacy screen and still cannot see an enemy's
territory. And it is the setting's cruellest joke: **you can hear the whole galaxy and you cannot
go anywhere.** Families separated by the Unarriving can talk. They cannot meet. Some have been
talking for seventy-four years.

An order to your own fleet travels the Whisper instantly. The fleet still takes seasons to arrive.
Command is fast; movement is not; that gap is the whole texture of the war.

## IV · Matter

**12. Metal is bulk.** Dug, smelted, spent. Nothing mysterious. It builds hulls and structures.

**13. Crystal refines into reckoning, and reckoning is consumed.**
It is burned to compute a blind jump, to run a probe, to fund an agent. It does not recharge and
cannot be recovered from a completed transit. This is why the navigational economy is an
*extraction* economy, and why every empire is structurally forced to keep expanding.

**14. The Trellis and the Lamps were made of the same substance.**
Unproven, universally suspected, and the Crystalline Entity is made of it too. Never confirmed on
screen.

**15. Nothing regenerates for free.** Two exceptions, both with stated mechanisms and no others
permitted:
- **Bioform hulls grow.** They are alive, they are raised in nutrient berths over nine years, and
  they mature. A veteran hull is an older animal. Replacement takes a decade — no surge production,
  ever.
- **Mechanicus capitals self-repair.** Internal fabrication stock and automated plate-work, capital
  hulls only, because only a capital has the volume to carry a foundry. It consumes stock, and the
  stock runs out.

**16. Terraforming takes generations. LOCKED.** The tech ladder does not change a world in a
season; it makes a world *reachable* by people willing to spend three lifetimes on it. Every
terraform level is somebody's grandchildren.

---

## V · Life and death

**17. Death is permanent.** No resurrection, no cloning of persons, no uploading, no
consciousness transfer. Anywhere. This is what makes a manifest matter.

**18. The Silicon Collective is not made of dead people.** It is a native artificial civilisation
that arose on its own. It has no human ancestry, no uploaded founders, no tragic origin. Getting
this wrong turns them into a cliché.

**19. Nobody is telepathic.** The Zephyr Swarm is a *networked* intelligence using ordinary
signal, which is why a cut connection is permanent and why the Remainder exists. It is bandwidth,
not mysticism.

---

## VI · The three that look like magic

**20. The Crystalline Entity are silicate life.** Slow metabolism driven by radiation and mineral
uptake. They do not phase, teleport, possess, or communicate at range. Their durability is
material science and their poor metal output is because mining metal on their world means digging
through people. **They cannot be quickly killed and they cannot quickly do anything.**

**21. Quantum positional uncertainty is short-range and bounded. LOCKED.**
A Quantum hull's position is genuinely indeterminate within a small radius. Hard limits:
- It cannot exceed **one sector**. It is not map-wide teleportation.
- It cannot phase out of a **hazard roll**. A shoal does not care where you probably are.
- It cannot phase **into held space** past defences.
- It cannot phase a hull that is **carrying** anything that must arrive intact.
Everything else about them is exotic manufacturing, which is why their hulls cost 30% more.

**22. Void Walker speed is engineering plus inherited memory. No prescience.**
They are fast because their hulls are built for nothing else, and they know routes because their
couriers memorised them. They have no foresight, cannot see into lanes, and **cannot outrun a
hazard they did not already know about.** Wren survived by breaking doctrine, not by being
gifted.

---

## VII · The one thing nobody can do

**23. No one can build a Lamp.**

Every empire has a programme. All of them are within about ten per cent of the same research, and
none is close. Completing the tech tree gets you to the *threshold* — which is what Scientific
Victory means and why the Galactic Wonder is a Lamp.

**24. The shutdown is still propagating. LOCKED.**
The failure front is an expanding shell that began in BU 61 and will complete in roughly nine
hundred years. There is still-lit space outside it. It is receding faster than anyone can travel.
The Ancients started this and cannot stop it, which is why their silence is futility rather than
stubbornness.

**25. What is behind the quarantine is never designed, named, or shown.**
Not in a codex, not in art, not in a cutscene, not in a stat block. We show effects — a docking
schedule with a gap in it — and nothing else. The moment it has a shape it becomes a monster, and
a monster is far less frightening than an absence.

This is the hardest rule to keep and the most important one.
