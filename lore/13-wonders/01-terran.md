# Terran Empire — The Concord Lamp

*Numbers marked **[P]** are placeholders pending a balance pass.*

---

## The Wonder: **The Concord Lamp**

The canonical one. The Wonder the victory condition was always about.

Terra does not invent it. That is the whole point and it must never be softened: they rebuild a Lamp
from the **archive** — from Concord maintenance schedules, tolerance tables, a shipwright's
apprenticeship syllabus, and eleven cross-referenced requisition forms for parts nobody can now name.
Nobody on Terra understands why it works. They understand the *documentation*, and the documentation
is complete enough, and this is the most Terran achievement imaginable: the greatest engineering
project since the Concord, delivered by filing.

It is built at a Gate — one of the eleven dead ports — and the choice of which is the single most
consequential domestic political decision in Terran history. Nine constituencies. One Lamp.

**What it does [P].** Lights one lane between two sectors you hold. Fleets moving along a lit lane
take **no hazard rolls of any kind** and pay **no movement cost**. The lane can be extended one
sector at a time by further construction, and every extension is another turn of work and another
increment of upkeep.

It is the only effect in the game that suspends Law 1, and it suspends it for exactly one route,
because a lit lane is not blind.

**Maintenance [P].** Reckoning, continuously, at a rate that scales with lane length. A Lamp burns
crystal forever, and a long Lamp burns a lot of it. Terra's economy is average at everything, which
means the Lamp is affordable and nothing else is.

**How it dies.** Take or raze the Gate that houses it. The lane goes dark instantly along its whole
length, and any fleet in transit on it that turn is *in the open, blind, mid-crossing* — and takes
transit rolls for every sector it was passing through. **Destroying a Concord Lamp kills whatever is
using it**, which is the same thing that happened in AU 0, at a scale of one lane, and every admiralty
in the galaxy has noticed the symmetry.

**The danger.** This is the mistake in its purest form. A working Lamp is a road, and roads run both
ways, and the Ancients turned them off for a reason. Terra will relight the galaxy out of
custodianship, carefully, with excellent records, and it will be the most conscientious catastrophe in
history.

## Signature technologies

**1 · Registry Standardisation** *(Economy)*
Every sector you hold contributes a small permanent research trickle from filed survey returns, and
your traces never degrade. Nobody else's knowledge outlives their veterans; yours outlives everybody.
> *Filed is not the same as understood. It is, however, the same as kept.*

**2 · Certified Traces** *(Intel)*
Your probes cannot be salted. A false trace fails against Terran navigation because every route is
cross-referenced against two independent filings before a hull is committed — which is slow,
expensive, and the reason enemy counter-intelligence simply does not work on you.
> *We have been lied to before. We wrote that down as well.*

**3 · Concord Reconstruction** *(Orbital — capstone, Wonder prerequisite)*
Not a discovery. A *reading*. Eleven documents, cross-referenced over four decades, assembled into a
buildable specification by people who cannot explain a single line of it.
> *Cabinet forty-one, drawers nine through nineteen. It was always there.*

## Signature hulls

**Cutter, *Diligence* class** — Light, fast, unarmed, and carries a certified duplicate of your chart.
If a Diligence survives an engagement in a sector, **no trace knowledge is lost there even if the rest
of the fleet dies.** Terra's answer to the Void Walker problem: the copy comes home.

**Tender, *Ganges* class** — Field engineering hull. Can raise an orbital turret in a held sector with
no spaceport, and repairs orbital structures in place. Unglamorous, indispensable, and the reason
Terran territory is the hardest in the galaxy to dislodge once it has been held for a season.

**Ledger Ship, *Assurance* class** — Command capital. While present, every friendly fleet in the
sector fights to filed doctrine: a modest combined-arms bonus that applies only if the fleet contains
at least three hull classes. It does nothing for a monoculture fleet, on purpose. Somebody is keeping
the record, and the record rewards doing it properly.

## Notes

**Why the Lamp is Terra's and not everyone's.** Eleven other empires have Lamp programmes and all of
them are trying to *understand* the thing. Terra is the only one that stopped trying and started
reading, and it works, and it is the most damning comment the setting makes about genius.

**Campaign.** This is mission 9, "Relight" — a long fixed-position build under general assault, and
the beat that matters is that **it works**: one lane lights, a fleet crosses it and arrives with no
losses at all, and Rell reports a clean crossing at a scale nobody has heard in three generations. It
is the best moment in the game, and mission 10 takes it away.

**Access check.** Nothing here grants Terra a capability it lacks, because Terra lacks none. Their
signature hulls are deliberately *supporting* rather than superior — a courier, an engineer, and a
staff ship. Terra's Wonder is enormous and Terra's ships are still nobody's best, which is correct.
