# Crystalline Entity — The First Thickness

*Numbers marked **[P]** are placeholders pending a balance pass.*

---

## The Wonder: **The First Thickness**

The Crystalline Wonder is a person.

They are made of the same substance as the Lamps. Everyone suspects it; the Standing has felt it since
AU 0. So their Wonder is not a machine and not a road. It is a **volunteer** — an elder, one of the
oldest and largest thicknesses, consenting to be restructured, permanently and consciously, into
something that does what a Lamp does.

They do not die. That is the part that matters, and it is the part nobody else can bear. The elder
remains present, aware, and slow, for as long as the Wonder stands, and it cannot be undone, and the
Standing has a word for the state that translates as *given*.

The first volunteer was Elder Sarn. This is proposed, not locked, and it is the single most powerful
narrative option in this folder: the witness who told Ilsa Rell that the galaxy was **set down** gently
by something with manners, choosing seventy-four years later to become the thing that sets it back up.

**What it does [P].** A permanent **sphere** of safety, not a corridor. Every sector within a radius of
the Wonder is hazard-free and permanently visible, for you and for anyone you permit. It is smaller than
a Concord Lamp's reach and it is a *volume* rather than a line, and inside it there is no dark at all.

**Maintenance.** Not resources. **Consent.**

Every so often the Wonder requires another volunteer, and the requirement is announced, and the Standing
must agree. This is a *political* upkeep — the only one in the game — and the Whole faction has been
winning for seventy years. The Crystalline Wonder is maintained by the ongoing willingness of a people
to give up their elders, and it will eventually be refused.

**How it dies.** It can be attacked, and attacking it is the most reputationally catastrophic act
available in the game — every other empire's diplomatic posture toward the attacker should change,
permanently. Or it can simply **run out of volunteers**, in which case nobody did anything wrong and it
goes dark anyway.

**The danger.** It works. And the moment it works, everyone in the galaxy understands what the Trellis
was made of — that the roads they inherited were somebody's given elders, thousands of them, and that the
Standing did not invent this practice so much as *recover* it. The Crystalline Wonder does not reopen
the road. It answers the question nobody had the courage to ask.

## Signature technologies

**1 · Grown Deflection** *(Shields)*
Your shields regenerate between rounds of an engagement. Nothing else in the galaxy does this, because
nothing else in the galaxy has shields that are *tissue*. A Crystalline fleet in a long battle is
harder in round four than round one. Per Law 4 this still does nothing against a shoal.
> *You are wearing us down. We would like you to consider how long that will take.*

**2 · The Yielding Cut** *(Economy)*
A refinement of inert-quarrying that raises crystal income substantially. It is also the Yielding
faction's position rendered as a technology, and every level of it strengthens the Whole's argument.
> *These were nobody. We are almost certain.*

**3 · Consenting Structure** *(Shields — capstone, Wonder prerequisite)*
The formalism for making a living thickness permanent infrastructure without ending it. It took forty
years to derive and eleven years to agree to.
> *We asked whether it could be done. Then we asked whether it should be. The second question took
> longer.*

## Signature hulls

**Standing** — Grown, not built. The most durable hull in the game: enormous, slow, shield-heavy, and
armed with something that is part of its body and cannot be upgraded. It cannot carry missiles and never
will. It does not win fights quickly. It wins them eventually.

**Terrace** — A hull that is nearly a fortification. It moves once, anchors a sector, and thereafter
functions as a fixed defensive structure with a shield envelope that extends to friendly hulls in the
sector. The Standing's answer to the problem of holding ground: send something that will simply stay.

**Ringer** — Its weapon is resonance. A Ringer strike does modest damage on impact and then **continues
to damage the target in subsequent rounds** as the tone propagates through the hull. It is the only
damage-over-time weapon in the setting, and its origin is the ringing crust of Sarn's World, where a
struck note holds for hours.

## Notes

**Access check.** `WEAPONS: 2` and `MISSILES: 0` hold. The Ringer is not a missile and not a weapons-tech
uplift — it is a hull-integral resonance armament, formidable and, in keeping with the race, entirely
unimprovable. `ARMOR: 2` holds; everything here is Shields.

**Why this Wonder matters most thematically.** Eleven Wonders are about roads. This one is about what
roads are *made of*, and it retroactively makes the reckoning market — eleven empires buying refined
fragments of a living people to fund their navigation — into something much worse than a tasteless
trade. The Trellis was not built out of crystal. It was built out of volunteers.

Never confirm this on screen. The Wonder implies it. The Ancients will not discuss it. That is enough.
