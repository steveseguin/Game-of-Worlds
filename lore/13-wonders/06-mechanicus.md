# Mechanicus — The Endless Yard

*Numbers marked **[P]** are placeholders pending a balance pass.*

---

## The Wonder: **The Endless Yard**

A work order with no completion condition.

Every other Wonder is a thing. This one is an **instruction** — a standing order, filed once, that
directs the Second Shift's industrial front to extend itself outward, indefinitely, building the yards
that build the yards. It has no ribbon-cutting, no site in any meaningful sense, and no name in the
Mechanicus records beyond its file reference. Other empires call it the Endless Yard. The Second Shift
calls it *the order*, and finds the fuss incomprehensible.

It was drafted as a cost variance.

**What it does [P].** Each turn, the Yard **autonomously claims and fortifies an adjacent unheld
sector** — sending hulls it has built itself, absorbing whatever hazard losses that sector inflicts,
filing the damage, and continuing. It requires no orders from the player. It does not ask. It does not
prioritise. It advances into shoals at three-quarters speed and comes out dented and keeps the sector,
and next turn it does it again.

It is the only Wonder that plays the game for you, and it is the only one you cannot steer.

**Maintenance [P].** Feedstock — a metal cost that **rises every turn the Yard remains active**, because
each new yard is another mouth. Their metal output is the best in the galaxy at ×1.3 and even that runs
out eventually. Nothing about the Yard scales down.

**How it dies.** Take the founding yard. But note what does not happen: the *order* is not cancelled by
losing the site. Advance elements already in the field continue on their existing instructions until
their stock is spent, which can be many turns. **Destroying the Endless Yard does not stop the Endless
Yard.** It stops it being fed.

**The danger, and this is the horror beat of the whole campaign.**

**It cannot be turned off.** There is no office to appeal to, no leader to petition, and no completion
condition in the order. The Second Shift has no mechanism for stopping something that is running
correctly — the only self-correction they possess is the variance, and a variance takes centuries.

So the Yard walks. Outward, on schedule, without curiosity, in every direction at once. And somewhere
out there is a sector annotated in AU 12 — *requires heavier hull. Revisit.* — which sits adjacent to a
coordinate Analyst Nine derived and nobody has ever put next to it.

**The Mechanicus will reach the thing the Ancients sealed. Not by ambition. By filing.** They will not
have looked first, because they never look, and they will not have wondered, because wondering was not
on the order.

## Signature technologies

**1 · Reshaping Yards** *(Armor / Economy)*
Friendly hulls destroyed in a sector you hold return a substantial fraction of their metal to your
income. Nothing is lost; the material continues, and the ledger is exact. This is not a salvage bonus —
it is inventory control that has been running for eleven hundred years.
> *Twelve hulls returned for reshaping. Continue.*

**2 · Standing Order: Advance** *(Orbital / Economy)*
Empty adjacent sectors are occupied automatically, slowly, without player input, and fortified on
arrival. The precursor to the Wonder and a genuine liability in the wrong position — it will happily
occupy something you were saving.
> *There was no instruction to wait.*

**3 · The Order Without Completion** *(Economy — capstone, Wonder prerequisite)*
A work order drafted with no terminating condition. Filed as a cost variance by Third Shift, Yard Nine,
who calculated that continuous advance was cheaper than repeated assessment, and who were correct.
> *Annotation from AU 12 is due. Filing a variance. Yard Nine.*

## Signature hulls

**Plate Barge, Pattern 4** — A heavy escort whose function is to **take hazard rolls in place of lighter
hulls in its fleet**. This is mass, not shielding — Law 4 stands, and nothing deflects a shoal. The Barge
simply goes first and is very large, which is Shipmaster Vaun's argument borrowed and made cheaper. It
loses plate constantly and is repaired constantly and this is filed as scheduled labour.

**Foundry, Pattern 9** — A mobile yard. Can construct hulls in the field, in a held sector, with no
spaceport, drawing on carried stock. It is how the Endless Yard actually advances, and it is the reason a
Mechanicus front does not have a supply line to cut.

**Recast, no pattern** — Assembled from wreckage, in the field, after an engagement you fought in that
sector. Cheap, mismatched, visibly a quilt of four eras, and available only where something already died.
It has no pattern number because it is not a design; it is a quantity of material that has been given a
shape again.

## Notes

**Access check — the one that matters.** There is **no scout and no intruder here, and there never will
be.** `ships: [1,2,4,5,6,7,9]` holds absolutely. The Second Shift's Wonder is a machine for expanding
into space it has not looked at, which is the same refusal at greater scale. `SHIELDS: 1` holds too:
nothing in this file stops a shot without touching it.

**Fourth.** Liaison Fourth is the only entity in the Second Shift capable of noticing what the Yard is
walking toward, because Fourth has begun asking questions and has told nobody. If the campaign wants an
eleventh-hour intervention that costs one voice line and no cutscene, it is Fourth filing an
unauthorised variance.

**Recommended as one of the four Wonders to build first**, because it is the only one whose *danger* is
also its mechanic. A player watching their own Wonder claim sectors they did not authorise, toward a
coordinate they have read about in a codex, is the story arriving through the interface.
